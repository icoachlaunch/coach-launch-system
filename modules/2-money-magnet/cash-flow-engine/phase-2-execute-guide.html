What This Funnel Does

Phase 2 is the Free Masterclass funnel — still free to attend, but it monetises the flow, built for coaches in the Execute phase of the Accelerator.

The class is free, so it fills easily. The difference from the Workshop is what happens around it: a VIP upgrade offered the moment they register, and a paid strategy session offered after they've seen you teach. So the funnel starts generating cash and qualifying buyers before you ever make the core pitch.

▲ Which phase am I on?

Your Money Model tells you where you are in the Accelerator — build the funnel that fits where you're at. Move to Execute once the free Workshop is filling and you're ready to add paid upgrades to the flow.

The Funnel — 6 Pages, In Order

Each page hands off to the next. This is the sequence:

  1. 1

    Free Masterclass Registration

    The opt-in. Captures the registrant and sets the promise of the class.

  2. 2

    VIP Upsell

    Immediately after registering, offers a paid VIP upgrade — a better seat, bonuses, deeper access. The funnel's first paid step.

  3. 3

    Confirmation

    Confirms the registration and any VIP purchase, and sets the show-up — date, time, how to attend.

  4. 4

    Event Gate

    The access page they come through on the day to get into the live room.

  5. 5

    Replay

    For anyone who missed it or wants to rewatch — carrying the same call-to-action to the next step.

  6. 6

    PASS Session

    The paid strategy session offer — a low-ticket paid audit / strategy call that turns an attendee into a high-intent, paying lead and your next conversation.

How To Build It

You don't hand-code these pages — you build them in GHL (GoHighLevel) AI Studio, and the Cash Flow Engine™ Funnel Builder writes the build prompts for you. You keep the proven template structure; it gets your brand, your event, and your copy.

Prep your assets

Have your Event Magnet™ (the hook and core copy for the event), your Money Model (the offer and prices, including the VIP and the paid session), your Business Brand Profile (host details), and your Brand Kit theme ready. The builder reads from these — it never makes anything up.

Run the Funnel Builder skill

Tell it you're building Phase 2 · Execute. It reads the six templates and your assets and produces a GHL AI Studio build prompt for each page, in order.

Build each page in GHL AI Studio

Give GHL AI Studio the template as the structure, paste the prompt, and it builds the page in your brand with your real copy. Work through the six pages in sequence.

Wire the funnel

Connect the pages in order inside GHL and set the order form on the VIP upgrade and the paid session. The pages carry no payment logic — GHL's own form handles that.

See the templates first in the Funnel Templates gallery — open the Execute box to preview each page.

Is It Ready?

No score — it's live when it flows. It's done when:

  • Your Event Magnet™ and Money Model are built — the funnel reads from them.
  • All six pages are built in GHL and connected in order.
  • The VIP upgrade and the PASS session each have their order form wired (GHL handles payment).
  • Registration → VIP → confirmation → event access all flow without a dead end.
  • The replay points to the PASS session so late viewers still convert.
