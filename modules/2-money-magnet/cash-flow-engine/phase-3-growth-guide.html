What This Funnel Does

Phase 3 is the Paid Masterclass funnel — the attendee buys a ticket to be there, built for coaches in the Growth phase of the Accelerator.

Charging for the seat changes everything: it filters for buyers, pays for your traffic, and fills the room with people who show up ready. On top of the paid ticket sits a VIP upgrade and a paid strategy session, and the funnel ends on a membership offer — so a one-time buyer becomes recurring, monthly income.

▲ Which phase am I on?

Your Money Model tells you where you are in the Accelerator — build the funnel that fits where you're at. Move to Growth once your free events convert reliably and you're ready to sell the seat and add recurring revenue.

The Funnel — 7 Pages, In Order

Each page hands off to the next. This is the sequence:

  1. 1

    Paid Masterclass Registration

    The paid opt-in. Sells the ticket to the masterclass and captures the buyer.

  2. 2

    VIP Upsell

    Right after purchase, offers a VIP upgrade — a better experience and bonuses stacked on the ticket.

  3. 3

    PASS Upsell

    Offers the paid strategy session as a one-click upsell in the buying flow — no re-entering payment.

  4. 4

    Thank-You

    Confirms the purchase and every upgrade taken, and sets the show-up — date, time, how to attend.

  5. 5

    Event Gate

    The access page they come through on the day to get into the live room.

  6. 6

    Masterclass Replay

    For anyone who missed it or wants to rewatch — carrying the offer forward.

  7. 7

    Membership Offer

    The recurring offer — monthly or annual — that turns a one-time buyer into an ongoing member. This is the phase's engine of monthly income.

How To Build It

You don't hand-code these pages — you build them in GHL (GoHighLevel) AI Studio, and the Cash Flow Engine™ Funnel Builder writes the build prompts for you. You keep the proven template structure; it gets your brand, your event, and your copy.

Prep your assets

Have your Event Magnet™ (the hook and core copy for the event), your Money Model (the ticket price, the VIP, the paid session, and the membership pricing), your Business Brand Profile (host details), and your Brand Kit theme ready. The builder reads from these — it never makes anything up.

Run the Funnel Builder skill

Tell it you're building Phase 3 · Growth. It reads the seven templates and your assets and produces a GHL AI Studio build prompt for each page, in order.

Build each page in GHL AI Studio

Give GHL AI Studio the template as the structure, paste the prompt, and it builds the page in your brand with your real copy. Work through the seven pages in sequence.

Wire the funnel

Connect the pages in order inside GHL and set the order form on the paid registration, the VIP upgrade, the paid session, and the membership. The pages carry no payment logic — GHL's own form handles that.

See the templates first in the Funnel Templates gallery — open the Growth box to preview each page.

Is It Ready?

No score — it's live when it flows. It's done when:

  • Your Event Magnet™ and Money Model are built — the funnel reads from them.
  • All seven pages are built in GHL and connected in order.
  • The paid registration, VIP upsell, PASS upsell and membership each have their order form wired (GHL handles payment).
  • Purchase → VIP → PASS → thank-you → event access all flow without a dead end.
  • The replay and the funnel both point to the membership offer.
