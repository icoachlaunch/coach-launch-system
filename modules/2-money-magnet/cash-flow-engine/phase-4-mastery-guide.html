What This Funnel Does

Phase 4 is the Paid Challenge funnel — a multi-day event that does the selling for you, built for coaches in the Mastery phase of the Accelerator.

A challenge runs over several days, so people commit, do the work, and see a result before you ever pitch. That's why it carries the fullest stack: a paid seat, a VIP upgrade, a paid strategy session, an add-on, and a downsell to catch anyone who passed — and it ends on a high-ticket sales page. By the time that page loads, the room has already decided.

The Funnel — 9 Pages, In Order

Each page hands off to the next. This is the sequence:

  1. 1

    Paid Challenge Registration

    The paid opt-in for the multi-day challenge. Sells the seat and captures the buyer.

  2. 2

    VIP Upsell

    Right after purchase, offers a VIP upgrade — a better experience and bonuses on top of the seat.

  3. 3

    PASS Upsell

    Offers the paid strategy session as a one-click upsell in the buying flow.

  4. 4

    Add-On Upsell

    A one-click add-on that stacks more value onto the order — no re-entering payment.

  5. 5

    Downsell

    A lower-priced recovery offer for anyone who passed on the upsell, so no one leaves the flow empty-handed.

  6. 6

    Thank-You

    Confirms the purchase and the full stack taken, and sets the show-up for the challenge.

  7. 7

    Challenge Replay

    The replay for each session — for anyone who missed a day or wants to rewatch — carrying the challenge and the offer forward.

  8. 8

    Event Gate

    The access page they come through each day to get into the live sessions.

  9. 9

    High-Ticket Sales

    The long-form sales page for your core high-ticket program — the destination the whole challenge is built to earn. This is where the $100K Day is made.

How To Build It

You don't hand-code these pages — you build them in GHL (GoHighLevel) AI Studio, and the Cash Flow Engine™ Funnel Builder writes the build prompts for you. You keep the proven template structure; it gets your brand, your event, and your copy.

Prep your assets

Have your Event Magnet™ (the hook and core copy for the challenge), your Money Model (the seat price, the VIP, the paid session, the add-on, the downsell and the high-ticket offer), your Business Brand Profile (host details), and your Brand Kit theme ready. The builder reads from these — it never makes anything up.

Run the Funnel Builder skill

Tell it you're building Phase 4 · Mastery. It reads the nine templates and your assets and produces a GHL AI Studio build prompt for each page, in order.

Build each page in GHL AI Studio

Give GHL AI Studio the template as the structure, paste the prompt, and it builds the page in your brand with your real copy. Work through the nine pages in sequence.

Wire the funnel

Connect the pages in order inside GHL and set the order form on the paid registration and every upsell, add-on and downsell. The pages carry no payment logic — GHL's own form handles that.

See the templates first in the Funnel Templates gallery — open the Mastery box to preview each page.

Is It Ready?

No score — it's live when it flows. It's done when:

  • Your Event Magnet™ and Money Model are built — the funnel reads from them.
  • All nine pages are built in GHL and connected in order.
  • The paid registration and every upsell, add-on and downsell have their order form wired (GHL handles payment).
  • Purchase → VIP → PASS → add-on → (downsell) → thank-you → event access all flow without a dead end.
  • The replay and the funnel both point to the high-ticket sales page.
