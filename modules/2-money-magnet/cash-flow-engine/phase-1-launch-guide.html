The simplest event funnel of the four — a free workshop that fills the room, delivers, and books your first strategy calls. This is where Launch-phase coaches start.

What This Funnel Does

Phase 1 is the Workshop funnel — the least complex of the four, built for coaches in the Launch phase of the Accelerator.

It's a free workshop: someone registers, shows up, gets value, watches the replay if they missed it, and books a call with you. No paid tickets, no upsell stack — the whole job of this funnel is to get people in the room and into a conversation.

▲ Which phase am I on?

Your Money Model tells you where you are in the Accelerator — build the funnel that fits where you're at, or whichever you're comfortable running. Launch is the natural starting point.

The Funnel — 6 Pages, In Order

Each page hands off to the next. This is the sequence:

  1. 1

    Free Workshop Registration

    The opt-in. Captures the registrant and sets the promise of the workshop.

  2. 2

    Ask Campaign

    A short "what's your biggest question?" step that tells you what your audience most wants — and feeds your workshop and your pitch.

  3. 3

    Thank-You

    Confirms the registration and sets the show-up — dates, what to expect, how to attend.

  4. 4

    Event Gate

    The access page they come through on the day to get into the live room.

  5. 5

    Replay

    For anyone who missed it or wants to rewatch — with the same call-to-action driving to the next step.

  6. 6

    Application / Booking

    Books the strategy call. This is the point of the whole funnel — turn a workshop attendee into a conversation.

How To Build It

You don't hand-code these pages — you build them in GHL (GoHighLevel) AI Studio, and the Cash Flow Engine™ Funnel Builder writes the build prompts for you. You keep the proven template structure; it gets your brand, your event, and your copy.

Prep your assets

Have your Event Magnet™ (the hook and core copy for the event), your Money Model (the offer and prices), your Business Brand Profile (host details), and your Brand Kit theme ready. The builder reads from these — it never makes anything up.

Run the Funnel Builder skill

Tell it you're building Phase 1 · Launch. It reads the six templates and your assets and produces a GHL AI Studio build prompt for each page, in order.

Build each page in GHL AI Studio

Give GHL AI Studio the template as the structure, paste the prompt, and it builds the page in your brand with your real copy. Work through the six pages in sequence.

Wire the funnel

Connect the pages in order inside GHL and set your booking / order form where needed. The pages carry no payment logic — GHL's own form handles that.

See the templates first in the Funnel Templates gallery — open the Launch box to preview each page.

Is It Ready?

No score — it's live when it flows. It's done when:

  • Your Event Magnet™ and Money Model are built — the funnel reads from them.
  • All six pages are built in GHL and connected in order.
  • Registration → thank-you → event access all flow without a dead end.
  • The replay and the application/booking page both point to your call booking.
A workshop with no funnel is a nice hour that goes nowhere. The funnel is what turns "that was helpful" into a booked call — and a booked call is where the $100K Day actually starts.
