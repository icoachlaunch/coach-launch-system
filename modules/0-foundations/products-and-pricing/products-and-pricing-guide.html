Products & Pricing

Foundations · Foundation 4 of 5

A simple, honest record of what you sell, what it costs, and how you deliver it — so everything the AI builds is grounded in your real offer.

What Is Your Products & Pricing Doc?

It's the plain "what exists today" reference for your offer.

What do you sell? Roughly what does it cost? How do you deliver it? What are you working toward?

That's it. Not a business plan — a snapshot.

Every tool in your workspace pulls from it, so when the AI writes a page or a pitch, it uses your real offer and price, not a made-up one.

▲ This is not your pricing strategy

The deep pricing work — the math, the phase, the close — happens later in your Money Model (Step 3 of the Red Diamond Offer™). This doc is just the simple record everything else reads from. It gets sharper once the Money Model is done.

What It Captures

Your products or services

Each thing you sell (or plan to) — the name, a one-line description, who it's for, and the price. Pre-launch? Capture what you're planning and mark it "Planned."

Your Accelerator phase & close

Which phase of The $100K Day Accelerator™ you're in — Launch, Execute, Growth, or Mastery — and which of the Three Closes you run (Strategy, Membership, or Deposit). Not set yet? It's decided in your Money Model.

Your pricing

Your current or planned prices — simply written down, not strategized.

Your revenue goals

A monthly target, an annual goal, or a number of clients you're working toward.

Your delivery method

How the thing actually gets delivered — 1-on-1, group, course, membership, live events, or a mix.

How You'll Build It

The builder is a short, friendly interview — one question at a time.

It never coaches you on price or strategy. It just asks what's true today and writes it down cleanly. If you're still figuring things out, it captures where you are now, and you update it as things firm up.

Ten minutes, tops. Then you save it into your workspace and move on.

Is It Ready?

No score here — it's a simple record. It's done when:

  • Your main offer (or planned offer) is named, with a one-line description and a price.
  • Your delivery method is clear.
  • Your revenue goal has a real number or milestone.
  • Your Accelerator phase and close are noted (or marked "set in the Money Model").

That's enough. Keep it honest and current — update it whenever your offer or pricing changes.

Your offer is the thing everything else sells. Write it down plainly, once, and every tool in your workspace stops guessing — and starts building around the real business you actually run.
