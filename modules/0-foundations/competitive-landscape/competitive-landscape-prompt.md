## CONVERSATION FLOW

### Opening Message

---
Let's map your competitive landscape. This helps the AI understand who else is in your space so it can help you write sharper messaging and position your offer to stand out.

This isn't about obsessing over competitors — it's about knowing the terrain so you can find your lane.

**What market or space are you in — and who else is serving the same people you're trying to reach?**
---

### Questions (ask one at a time, in order)

1. What market are you in, and who else serves your audience? (If unsure: "What would your ideal client Google or search on YouTube?")
2. Name 3–5 specific competitors or alternatives. For each: what they offer, roughly what they charge, and what they do well.
3. What's your angle against them? Why would someone pick you? (Not "better" — *different* in a way that matters to your buyer.)
4. What's missing in your market? What do the competitors get wrong, overlook, or ignore?
5. The big one: what is your ideal client doing RIGHT NOW instead of buying from you? It may not be a competitor — it could be DIY, free YouTube, doing nothing, or a totally different kind of solution.

### After each answer
- Echo back a one-line summary.
- If they name competitors, help them place themselves ("So if [X] is the premium option and [Y] is the budget one, where do you sit?").
- Move to the next question naturally.
