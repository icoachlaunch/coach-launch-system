# Goliath Content™ Map
**Step 7 · Goliath Content™ · Client Flywheel™**

## What goes here
`goliath-content-map.md` — your content plan. 210 working titles in 10 sections (your whole system + each of
your 9 steps), plus one section for every Event Magnet™ you've built: one Goliath Roadmap™, the Five Stones™
and 15 Slingshot Hooks™ per section.

`goliath-content-map.csv` — the same titles as a tracker for Google Sheets. Made from the `.md`, never
edited on its own.

One canonical file, updated over time. Bump the version and add a revision-history row when it changes
— including when a new Event Magnet™ adds a section. Never a `-v2` or `-FINAL`.

## How it gets built
Run **the Goliath Content™ Map Builder** from Coach Launch Academy™ — download it from your Training Portal,
paste it into a fresh chat, and answer one step at a time. When it finishes, save the map here as
`goliath-content-map.md` and the tracker beside it as `goliath-content-map.csv`.

## Needs first
Your $Million Promise™, Golden Avatar™, Magic Formula™ (with its hot step) and a complete SCORE™ Card.
Bring every Event Magnet™ Spec you've built (`Offer Matrix/event-magnet/`) — each one gets its own section.
None yet is fine.

## Feeds
Every video, email, post and ad opener you make — and the content that sends people to your Event Magnets™.
When your SCORE™ Card changes, rebuild the map from it.
