# Program Modules
**Step 7 · Goliath Content™ · Part 2 · Client Flywheel™**

## What goes here
`module-01-[slug].md` … `module-09-[slug].md` — your nine **Module Specs**, one per step of your Magic
Formula™, each named for its step (slug = the step name in lowercase, without "The" and without ™, words
joined by hyphens — `module-02-perfect-client-map.md`). Each spec is the master copy of that module: the
page (Part A · A1–A10) and the teach for your call or video (Part B · B1–B8).

The finished pages (step 2 of 2) save beside their specs — one per module, as the visual step exports them.

Nine canonical files, updated over time. Change the spec, bump its `version`, update `last_updated`.
Never a `-v2` or `-FINAL`.

## How it gets built
**Step 1 of 2 —** run **the Program Module Specs Builder** from Coach Launch Academy™: download it from your
Training Portal, paste it into a fresh chat, bring your documents, and confirm one module at a time. Save
each spec here as it's written.

**Step 2 of 2 —** **the Program Module Visual** turns the nine confirmed specs, your `BRAND KIT:` block and a
headshot into the finished pages in your brand. Open a new chat for it.

## Needs first
Your Magic Formula™ (required). Your Enrollment Doc (strongly recommended — your program's calls, tracker
and next-level offer come from it). Any notes, transcripts, recordings or slides of how you teach each
step. Your SCORE™ Card, $Million Promise™ and Golden Avatar™. Any Event Magnet™ Spec
(`../../Offer Matrix/event-magnet/`). Any member results.

## Feeds
Your program — the nine pages your members work through, in order, on your calls or in your course
platform. Each module's email/post angle (B6b) also sells the concept behind the step outside the program.

## Memory
`NOTES.md`, beside the asset — the decisions and why, what was rejected, what's still open, one line per session. Claude reads it before touching this asset and adds to it after every save (it makes the file the first time). Only the status line goes in `CONTEXT.md`; the reasoning stays here.
