# Step 5 · The Authority Detonator™
**Money Magnet™ · Pillar 2** — the video that plays the moment someone registers for your event

Your Authority Detonator™ is a 5–12 minute video on a Cash Flow Engine™ funnel page. Its job is authority for the
event: build rapport, get them excited about the day, and get them to **show up**. On the VIP upsell page, its job is
to get them to **upgrade**.

**The Builder writes the script. You record it.** Download the Authority Detonator™ Builder from your Training
Portal, paste it into a fresh chat, and run it — one video, one page, one job.

Save the finished scripts here:
- `authority-detonator-thank-you.md` — the thank-you page video (every phase). The ask: show up live.
- `authority-detonator-vip.md` — the VIP upsell page video (Execute, Growth, Mastery). The ask: upgrade to VIP.

One file per placement. One canonical copy of each — never a -v2. Re-run the Builder to update it.

**Needs first:** your Event Magnet™ Spec (Part B) and your Money Model. The VIP video also needs your Cash Flow Max™
Blueprint. Your SCORE™ Card makes it sharper; your Brand Voice makes it sound like you.

**Feeds:** the Cash Flow Engine™ Funnel Builder (the page it plays on) and the Genie X Converter™ (the messages
that point back to it).

## Memory
`NOTES.md`, beside the asset — the decisions and why, what was rejected, what's still open, one line per session. Claude reads it before touching this asset and adds to it after every save (it makes the file the first time). Only the status line goes in `CONTEXT.md`; the reasoning stays here.
