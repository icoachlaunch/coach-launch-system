The 3 Layers of AI — and the One Most People Miss

Most people only ever use the first three layers of AI:

1. Chat

You ask, it answers. Handy — but it forgets you the moment you close the tab.

2. Creation

It writes and makes things for you. Faster — but it still starts from nothing each time.

3. Agents

It takes actions and runs tasks. Powerful — but pointed at the wrong context, it just does the wrong thing faster.

All three share the same weakness: the AI doesn't know your business. So you keep feeding it the same context by hand.

4. The Systems Layer ← this is the one

One documented system that carries everything the AI needs — your voice, your offer, your frameworks, your numbers — into every session, automatically. This is what your AI Workspace is. Build it once, and every layer above it gets sharper.

How It's Put Together

Four things, and each one has a job. Open the kit and this is what you'll see.

100k-day-ai-workspace/ ├── README.md how to set it up — for you ├── CLAUDE.md the brain — how the AI runs the workspace, where things live, the rules ├── CONTEXT.md your business at a glance — the AI keeps it current ├── PROGRESS.md your build log — what's locked, what's next │ ├── Reference/ THE TRUTH — your five Foundation docs, plus brand-kit/ (your one Kit) ├── Client Engine/ THE ASSETS — one folder for everything you build, grouped by step 1 to 9 └── skills/ THE TOOLS — slides, funnel pages, your Magic Formula™ graphic

The four files at the top run the place. README.md is for you. CLAUDE.md is for the AI — it says where everything lives and how to behave. CONTEXT.md and PROGRESS.md are the memory: the AI updates them every time something gets built, so the next session starts already knowing.

Reference is the truth. Your five Foundation documents — who you are, how you look, how you sound, what you sell, who you're up against. Every builder reads from here and never asks you twice. Your Brand Kit lives in here too: the one file that decides your look everywhere.

Client Engine™ is where the things you build go. One folder per asset, grouped by the step that builds it — your $Million Promise™, your Magic Formula™, your SCORE™ Card, your Event Magnet™, your Sniper Presentation™ and so on, through all nine steps. Every folder has a README that says exactly what goes in it.

skills are the tools that build from your assets — your slide deck from your Script, your funnel pages from your Event Magnet™ and Money Model, your Magic Formula™ graphic from your system. You don't paste a skill; you tell Claude what you want and it opens the right one.

Where are the builders?

Not in the kit — on purpose. The builder prompts live in Coach Launch Academy™, your Training Portal, where they get improved over time.

When Claude says it's time to build something, you download that builder from the portal, paste it into the chat, and answer one question at a time. Claude saves the result into the right folder. Your folders never go stale when a builder improves.

Your Five Foundations Live in Reference

These are the five documents you build first. Each has its own builder in Coach Launch Academy™ — a short, guided interview, one question at a time — and each saves over its stub in Reference/.

  1. Business Brand Profile — what you believe, why you exist, where you're headed, where you operate.
  2. Visual Style Guide — your look. You pick one Brand Kit and install it in Reference/brand-kit/.
  3. Brand Voice — how you write and speak, so the AI sounds like you.
  4. Products & Pricing — what you sell, how you price it, how you deliver it.
  5. Competitive Landscape — your market, your competitors, and your edge.

Do them in order. Each one makes the next builder — and every Client Engine™ step after — sharper.

One Brand Kit, installed once

Your look isn't a list of colours you try to remember. It's a real file. In your Visual Style Guide you pick one of six Kits, save its file into Reference/brand-kit/tokens/, and every slide, page, ad and post reads from it.

That folder holds exactly one Kit. You can change it later — but you replace it, never keep two. Two Kits is two brands, and every tool has to guess which one is true.

Set It Up in Claude

Download the starter kit, unzip it, and pick your path.

Option A — Claude Code / Cowork (recommended)

1

Unzip the kit somewhere easy to find (your Documents folder is perfect).

2

Open Claude and point it at the unzipped folder.

3

Start a new chat and type this:

"I just set up my $100K Day AI Workspace. Read the CLAUDE.md file in this folder and walk me through the setup."

Claude reads the brain, checks your Reference docs, and starts building with you — one step at a time. When a builder finishes, Claude saves the file straight into the right folder and updates your CONTEXT and PROGRESS.

Option B — Claude Projects (claude.ai)

1

Go to claude.ai and create a new Project.

2

Open CLAUDE.md, copy all of it, and paste it in as the Project's instructions.

3

Upload every other file from the kit into the Project, then start a chat:

"I just set up my $100K Day AI Workspace. Use the instructions and walk me through the setup."

Claude outputs each finished document in the chat. You copy it, paste it over the matching file, and re-upload it.

The One Rule That Keeps It Clean

As your workspace grows, one rule keeps it from turning into a mess:

One file per asset. You update the same file over time — you never make a "-v2" or a "-FINAL." When you improve something, Claude bumps the version and keeps a little history right inside the file.

That's it. One promise, one profile, one voice, one Kit — each living in one place, always current.
