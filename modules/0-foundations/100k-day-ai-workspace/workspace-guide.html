The 3 Layers of AI — and the One Most People Miss

Most people only ever use the first three layers of AI:

1. Chat

You ask, it answers. Handy — but it forgets you the moment you close the tab.

2. Creation

It writes and makes things for you. Faster — but it still starts from nothing each time.

3. Agents

It takes actions and runs tasks. Powerful — but pointed at the wrong context, it just does the wrong thing faster.

All three share the same weakness: the AI doesn't know your business. So you keep feeding it the same context by hand.

4. The Systems Layer ← this is the one

One documented system that carries everything the AI needs — your voice, your offer, your frameworks, your numbers — into every session, automatically. This is what your AI Workspace is. Build it once, and every layer above it gets sharper.

What's Inside Your Workspace

Three parts work together.

1. The brain. A file called PROJECT-INSTRUCTIONS.md. It tells Claude how to run your workspace — how to start, what to load, and how to save your work so nothing gets lost.

2. Your Foundations. Five short documents about your business — the ground truth everything pulls from. You build these first (about an hour), and this starter kit gives you a guided builder for each one.

3. Your growing library. As you build each asset of The $100K Day Formula™ (each step has its own builder), you save it into the workspace. Over time, this folder becomes your entire business system — all in one place.

▲ This is a starter kit

It sets up your Foundations. The Formula assets get added as you build them — the AI creates the right folder and files each one for you. Your workspace grows with you.

Your Five Foundations

These are the five documents you build first. Each has its own builder in the kit — a short, guided interview, one question at a time.

Set It Up in Claude

Download the starter kit, unzip it, and pick your path.

Option A — Claude Code / Cowork (recommended)

1

Unzip the kit somewhere easy to find (your Documents folder is perfect).

2

Open Claude and point it at the unzipped folder.

3

Start a new chat and type this:

"I just set up my $100K Day AI Workspace. Read the PROJECT-INSTRUCTIONS.md file in this folder and walk me through the setup."

Claude reads the brain, checks your Foundations, and starts building with you — one step at a time. When a builder finishes, Claude saves the file straight into the right folder.

Option B — Claude Projects (claude.ai)

1

Go to claude.ai and create a new Project.

2

Open PROJECT-INSTRUCTIONS.md, copy all of it, and paste it into the Project's instructions.

3

Upload every other file from the kit into the Project, then start a chat:

"I just set up my $100K Day AI Workspace. Use the PROJECT-INSTRUCTIONS and walk me through the setup."

Claude outputs each finished document in the chat. You copy it, paste it over the matching stub file, and re-upload it.

The One Rule That Keeps It Clean

As your workspace grows, one rule keeps it from turning into a mess:

One file per asset. You update the same file over time — you never make a "-v2" or a "-FINAL." When you improve something, Claude bumps the version and keeps a little history right inside the file.

That's it. One promise, one profile, one voice — each living in one place, always current.
