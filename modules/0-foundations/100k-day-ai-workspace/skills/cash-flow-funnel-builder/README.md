# Cash Flow Engine™ Funnel Builder · V3
**Step 5 · Cash Flow Engine™ · Money Magnet™**

## What it does
Builds your complete event funnel for one Accelerator phase (Launch, Execute, Growth or Mastery, or a custom
page order; free or paid entry; workshop, masterclass or challenge) as **standalone HTML pages in your Brand
Kit** — one self-contained file per page — from your own assets: `CONTEXT.md`, the Event Magnet™, the Magic
Formula™, the Money Model, the SCORE™ Card, the Business Brand Profile and your photos.

It works **page by page, with your approval**: it reads back what it's building from and lists anything
missing, shows you the three "problems we solve" panels first, then builds each page and waits for your
OK before the next. Every build **ends on a Design board** of every page, for you to view and edit before
anything is published.

## How to install it
V3 installs from the zip **`cl-cash-flow-funnel-builder-v3.zip`**, in your Training Portal / Guidebook's
Cash Flow Engine™ chapter. Then open `SKILL.md` and run it as written.

## Needs first
Event Magnet™ · Money Model · Business Brand Profile · the `BRAND KIT:` block (+ the Magic Formula™, the
SCORE™ Card and the Enrollment Doc where they're built).

## Saves to
`Client Engine/Money Magnet/cash-flow-engine/phase-<N>-<name>/` (`phase-1-launch`, `phase-2-execute`,
`phase-3-growth`, `phase-4-mastery`): every page, `index.html` (the page map), `values.json` and
`BUILD-REPORT.md`. Overwritten on a rebuild — never `-v2`. Then `CONTEXT.md` is updated.
