Pick the look for your Magic Formula™

Your Magic Formula™ is the 3-phase, 9-step system only you deliver. This turns it into a graphic you can put in front of people.

What you're looking at

Below are three finished designs. Same system, same words, three different looks.

The example is a made-up baby-sleep coach. It's there so you can see how a real system sits inside each design, instead of guessing from a blank template.

Your job on this page is simple. Pick the one that feels like your brand.

How this works

  1. Choose a style

    Scroll through the three below and pick one. Go with the look, not the sample words — those get replaced with yours.

  2. Hand over your details

    The builder asks for your Magic Formula™, your brand colour, your fonts and your business name. It asks one thing at a time.

  3. Get your file

    You get one HTML file. Double-click it and it opens in any browser. Screenshot it for an ad, or put it on a page.

What you'll need before you start

  • Your locked Magic Formula™. Three phase names, nine step names, and the one-line outcome for each step. If it isn't built yet, build it first — this tool won't write it for you.
  • Your $Million Promise™. It goes on the graphic word for word.
  • Your brand colour and fonts. Straight from your Visual Style Guide. One colour is enough.
  • A mark for your system. Not your business logo — a small icon or mark that stands for this system on its own. It sits at the top, left of your system name. Send an SVG if you have one, a PNG if you don't. No mark yet? Say so, and you'll get a simple one built from your system's initials.
  • Your business name. It sits in the corner of the graphic.
  • Your call to action, and the link it goes to. A short line and a button label — "Ready for full nights?" and "Book a free sleep call". The link can point anywhere you like: a booking calendar, a checkout, an application form, a waitlist.
  • Your step icons. Optional — see below. There's a built-in set if you'd rather skip it.

About the icons

Every step gets its own small icon, sitting to the left of the step name. The icons on this page are placeholders.

You've got three ways to get yours.

Option 1

Send your own

Already have an icon set you use? Hand the files over and they go straight in. SVG works best, PNG is fine.

Option 2

Connect a library

Plug in an icon service like Streamline with your own account key. The builder picks nine that match your steps and shows you the list before it uses them.

Option 3

Use the built-in set

Plain line icons that sit quietly next to any brand. Nothing to buy, nothing to set up. This is the default.

Either way, you approve every icon. The builder shows you all nine picks with the step each one belongs to, and you can swap any of them before it builds.

One more thing to know

Your step outcomes are written as full sentences. That's right for a document, but too long for a graphic — nine long sentences turn into a wall of text nobody reads.

So the builder will show you a shorter version of each outcome and ask you to approve it. Your words, just tighter.

It won't shorten anything without showing you first.
