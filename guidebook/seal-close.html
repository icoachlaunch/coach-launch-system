02

Why It Works

The closes differ by where the conversation sits next to the sale. In The SEAL™ Close, it sits after the deposit.

They pay, and then you talk, on the onboarding call. It's the only close where nobody needs a conversation to say yes.

That's the point. When every buyer goes through a sales call, you've added a bottleneck. Every day between the room and the call is a day they cool off.

The principle

The SEAL™ Close takes the gap out. The decision and the first payment happen in the same room.

It rides both laws, at full stretch

Immersion = Conversion. The longer people spend with you, the easier the close and the higher the offer you can put in front of them. Phase 4 is a multi-day event, the deepest immersion on the ladder. That's why it can carry your highest offer.

People who pay, pay attention. Money in before the close buys a better audience. Phase 4 runs the full money-in stack: the ticket, two bumps, a two-option VIP, a PASS™, Upsell 2 and downsells. By the close, the people in front of you have already said yes to you with their money.

What replaces the sales call

In the other closes, a conversation does some of the selling. Here, nothing does. So the work has to happen somewhere else.

  • The presentation carries everything. The price, the time, "will this work for me", "I need to talk to my partner". If your Sniper Presentation™ doesn't raise and answer them, nobody will.
  • The proof is undeniable. Real numbers, real people, real results. People hand over money without a conversation when what they've seen makes the decision obvious.
  • The enrolment is flawless. Nobody had a sales call to reassure them, so the process has to do it.
  • The urgency is real. A deposit buys scarcity. If nothing is genuinely limited, it has nothing to hold.

04

The Ask

The value stack is walked, the anchor is set, the price is out. Now the ask, and it's a short one.

  1. State the full price first. The whole number, out loud, before you mention the deposit. Lead with the deposit and it reads as a trick. Lead with the price and the deposit becomes a courtesy.
  2. Name the deposit and what it holds. The amount, that it comes off the total, and what it buys them: a real place, price or bonus. "It starts your payments" is not a reason to pay today.
  3. Say the terms plainly. When the balance is due (always before it starts), how it's paid, and that the deposit is non-refundable. In the room, never in small print.
  4. Give the steps, then stop talking. Numbered, on screen. Pay the deposit, you're in, and your onboarding call is next. This close lives or dies in the silence.
How it sounds

"The full investment is [full price]. That's what it costs to do this with me."

"You don't need all of it today. [Deposit amount] holds your place in the cohort, and it comes straight off the total. So it's not extra. It's the first part of it."

"The balance is due before we start: [terms]. And to be straight with you, the deposit is non-refundable. It's a commitment fee. It's what makes the place yours instead of someone else's."

"Link's on the screen. Put your deposit down and you're in. I'll see you in there."

Setting the deposit

$997 is the recommended default, and you can set your own. Your number lives in your Money Model.

The shape that works is roughly 5–10% of the full price. Big enough that paying it means something. Small enough that someone who wants in can find it this week.

Too small and it stops filtering. Too big and it's just the price with extra steps.

Non-refundable helps you both

A refundable deposit isn't a commitment. It's a placeholder. People put it down to stop thinking about the decision, then change their mind three days later.

Non-refundable makes the decision real in the room, which is exactly where you want it made. It also protects the person who did commit: their place isn't being held open by someone still deciding.

Saying it out loud feels like it should cost you sales. It doesn't. The people it filters out were never going to pay the balance.

It comes off the total. Always.

The deposit is part of the price, not on top of it. Say so every time. The moment a deposit looks like an extra fee, this close stops working.

05

The Call

The SEAL™ Close ends on one call: the onboarding call. It comes after they've paid.

It's not a sales call. The decision has already been made, and the deposit was its first payment. The call's job is to get them started.

Before the call: the confirmation

The moment they pay, a confirmation goes out. Within minutes, not days. It carries:

  • The terms they agreed to, including that the deposit is non-refundable.
  • The balance date, before it starts.
  • Their start date.
  • Their onboarding call.
  • Exactly what happens next.

This is where the close leaks if you get it wrong. Somebody pays, hears nothing for four days, and starts wondering what they've done.

No call before the deposit

Not even a friendly one. The moment you add a conversation before the sale, you're running a different close, and you've put the bottleneck back.

If someone in your room needs a conversation to decide, that's The PASS™ Close. It's still available, and it's still in your funnel. Run it on purpose, not as a rescue.

Common Mistakes

✕ Running it too early

A coach with a small audience and no results asks a cold room for $997, sight unseen. Nobody moves, and they decide the close is broken.

✓ Earn it first

Use ASK™, JOIN™ and PASS™ while you build proof. This close works when the event and the results are already doing the convincing.

✕ Hiding the non-refundable term

It feels awkward, so you skip it. Someone asks for their money back a week later, you say no, and now they're telling everyone.

✓ Say it in the ask

Plainly, as a normal part of the terms. Stated clearly, it's fair. Discovered later, it's a betrayal.

✕ Fake scarcity

"Only five spots!" Then six people pay and everyone gets in. Every other number you quoted all day becomes suspect.

✓ A real limit, held

If you can genuinely take everyone, use a real price deadline or a real bonus instead.

✕ Silence after the payment

They pay and hear nothing for days. No sales call reassured them, and now there's no confirmation either.

✓ Confirm at once

Terms, balance date, start date and the onboarding call, within minutes. Automate it.

✕ "Or book a quick call"

The room goes quiet, so you soften it. Now you've got a close nobody trusts and a diary full of undecided people.

✓ Pick your close and hold it

Choose before you're on stage. If you want the conversation, run The PASS™ Close deliberately.

09

Is It Ready?

Pass or fail. This close is the least forgiving of the four.

  • Earned. You graduated Phase 3, and you have real results to show.
  • Priced. Full price, deposit and balance terms are all set in your Money Model.
  • Proportionate. The deposit means something: $997, or roughly 5–10% of the full price.
  • Stated. Non-refundable is said out loud in the ask, not buried.
  • Credited. The deposit comes off the total, and you say so.
  • Holding something. A real place, price or bonus that the deposit reserves.
  • Objection-complete. Your presentation answers everything a call would have.
  • Confirmed. An immediate confirmation carries the terms, the balance date, the start date and the onboarding call.

All eight? Your SEAL™ Close is ready, and you've taken the sales call out of your business without losing the sale.
