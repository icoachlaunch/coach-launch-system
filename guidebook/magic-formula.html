01

The System Only You Deliver

Your Magic Formula™ is the system that delivers on your $Million Promise™, packaged so it looks and feels like something only you can do.

Your $Million Promise™ said who you help and what they get. Your Magic Formula™ shows how you get them there.

It's a 3-phase, 9-step system with a name. Your way of getting results stops being "advice" and starts being a product.

Most coaches sound exactly the same. "I help people get healthy." "I help businesses grow." Those promises vanish in a crowd.

When someone sees your nine steps, with catchy names and real outcomes, they stop comparing you to other coaches. They compare your system to doing nothing. And doing nothing loses.

This is also where your marketing gets easy. Every step is a quick win, and every quick win is a post, an email or a talk.

Matthew White built Coach Launch on systems like this.

$24.6 Million in high-ticket sales generated and $5.2 Million in recurring client results delivered, all sitting on top of offers that were built, not winged.

What it is not

It's not a course curriculum. You're not teaching everything you know. You're mapping the path to the result.

It's not a list of every service you offer. Cram it all in and it stops being clear enough to sell.

It's not your pricing or your offer. That's Step 3, your Red Diamond Offer™.

And it's not your lead magnet. That's your Event Magnet™, built later from one hot step of this system.

The principle

If a competitor could paste your system onto their site and nobody would blink, it isn't yours yet.

03

How You'll Build It

You don't build a Magic Formula™ by brainstorming everything you do. You build it in order, one piece at a time.

First you confirm your promise and your business type. Then you name the system, capture your method and shape it into 3 phases and 9 steps.

Then you name every step, write a real outcome for each, and pick the hottest step.

$Million Promise™ · the what → Magic Formula™ · the how = 3 phases × 3 steps = 9 quick wins

Figure 03 · The shape of a Magic Formula™. The promise is the finish line. The system is the track.

Always 3 phases, 9 steps

Every Magic Formula™ has the same shape: three steps in each of three phases. It's the rule of threes: memorable, teachable and easy to sell.

Phase 1The first phase

Steps 1, 2 and 3

Phase 2The middle phase

Steps 4, 5 and 6

Phase 3The final phase

Steps 7, 8 and 9

The whole system solves the big problem, your $Million Promise™. Each phase solves a major piece of it.

Each step is a mini promiseOne smaller problem solved and one quick win delivered, the same way your $Million Promise™ is one big, specific win.. That's why every step can become a post, an email, a workshop or a lead magnet on its own.

The timeline has to match

Your nine steps must realistically deliver your promise inside its timeline. If it says "in 90 days," the nine steps add up to 90 days or less.

Add up the honest time for all nine. If it blows past your promise, tighten the steps or fix the timeline. Don't pretend the gap isn't there.

04

Know Your Business Type

Before you write a single outcome, you need to know what kind of business you have. It decides how your outcomes are written and what language fits.

Type 1Product Creator

You teach or build, and the client implements. Coaches, consultants, course creators, memberships, done-for-you.

Type 2Journey Professional

You guide a client through a high-stakes transition, with or for them. Attorneys, medical, financial advisors.

Type 3Hybrid

You do both. Career coaches, test prep, health coaches for a specific condition.

The fast way to tell: if you say "I teach them how to…" you're a Product Creator. If you say "I guide them through…" or "I represent them in…" you're a Journey Professional.

TypeYour step outcomes are…Language
Product CreatorAssets the client builds: documents, frameworks, scripts, plans"You": they implement
Journey ProfessionalMilestones reached, decisions made, positions protected"We" is correct: you do it with them
HybridEarly steps are milestones, later steps are deliverablesBoth, depending on the step
The most common mistake here

Forcing Product Creator rules onto a Journey Professional. A divorce attorney doesn't hand the client "an asset in a folder" at every step.

Force the wrong type and you invent fake deliverables that don't match how the business really works.

05

Name It All So It Sells

Four things get named or written: the system, the phases, the steps and the outcomes. Here's each one, and the trap that kills it.

1

The System NameWhat people tell their friends

Without a name you're "a coach who helps with X." With one, you're the creator of a system.

Where to start. The [Name] System you discovered in your $Million Moment™. Sometimes it's already the final name. Sometimes it points to a stronger one.

How to get yours right

Name the outcome, not the format. To a smart buyer, words like "funnel," "course" and "program" feel generic.

Pick a format that fits your style: the [Name] Method, System, Formula, Machine, Engine, Protocol (strong for Journey Professionals) or Blueprint.

✕ Commoditized

"Client Funnel Program." "Divorce Support Course."

✓ Outcome-focused

"The Booked-Out Blueprint." "The Strategic Divorce Method."

Then the subtitle

Your subtitle is your $Million Promise™ turned into marketing copy. Skip "I help [avatar]…" and use "How [avatar] [outcome], without [obstacles]."

The trap: naming the format you deliver in. Nobody tells a friend about a program. They tell them about a result.

2

The Phase NamesThree names that hang together

Your three phases need names that are consistent, memorable and hint at the progression.

The rule. All verbs or all nouns, never mixed. Mixing feels off, even if the reader can't say why.

How to get yours right

Alliteration is optional but powerful. "Message, Machine, Momentum" and "Design, Develop, Deploy" are easy to remember and repeat.

A useful starting point: Product Creators usually move from learning, to building, to launching. Journey Professionals move from assessing, to acting, to completing.

✕ Mixed

Foundation (noun), Build (verb), Success (noun).

✓ Consistent

Foundation, Framework, Fuel. All nouns.

The trap: naming each phase on its own instead of as a set.

3

The Step NamesNine names people want

Boring step names don't sell. "Define your avatar" sounds like homework. "The Bullseye Buyer" sounds like something you want.

The rule. Two to four words, intriguing, hinting at the benefit, and brandable.

How to get yours right

Run the click test: would someone click on this name if it were a free lead magnet? If not, make it more specific and more intriguing.

Journey Professionals too. "Initial case assessment" becomes "The Full Picture Review."

✕ Generic

"Define your customer." "Create your outline." "Build your funnel."

✓ Catchy

"The Bullseye Buyer." "The Brain Dump." "The Money Machine."

The trap: describing the task instead of the payoff.

4

The Step OutcomesA real win at the end of every step

Every step needs an outcome: the specific win at the end. What counts depends on your business type.

Product Creator. What do they HAVE at the end? An asset built, and a real quick win.

Journey Professional. What is TRUE now that wasn't before? A milestone reached, a decision made, a position established.

ScoreTypeExample
5Deliverable or milestone"Your complete financial discovery is documented and filed."
4Decision or specific awareness"Know exactly where you stand: legally, financially, personally."
3Vague awareness: sharpen it"Understand your options."
2A feeling: blocked"Feel confident about your case."
✕ A feeling

"Feel confident about your pricing." "Feel ready to launch."

✓ What's had or true

"Your pricing locked in. You know exactly what to charge."

The trap: feelings. They're blocked for every business type. Ask what they'd have, or what would be true, that makes them feel that way.

06

The Hard Rules

These keep a system clear enough to sell. The Builder holds you to every one.

  1. Exactly 3 phases and 9 steps. Not 2 phases, not 4, not 8 steps or 12.
  2. Phase names are all verbs or all nouns. Never mixed.
  3. Step names are 2–4 words, catchy and brandable.
  4. Every outcome is specific. No feelings, for any business type.
  5. The timeline matches the promise. All nine steps fit inside it, or you fix the gap.
  6. It's your method, never invented. If you don't have one yet, the Builder researches your market and says so. It never makes up a source or a number.

The eight quality filters

Run the whole system, and each step, through these. They're the difference between a system that sells and one that flops.

FilterWhat it means
SingularOne clear outcome, not a buffet of benefits
SequentialEach step leads logically to the next
EssentialSkip the step and the system breaks
TangibleYou can picture what they GET, or what's TRUE, at the end
CentralYour content and marketing can be built around it
Unique mechanismIt hints at YOUR way of doing it, not generic advice
Proven marketReal people already pay to solve this. Competition is validation
Hungry audienceA large, active crowd wants it, not an obscure niche

If you can't say what someone has, or what's true, at the end of a step, it isn't tangible enough yet. Sharpen it before you move on.

07

The Hot Step

You don't need the whole system live to start pulling people in. One step is the hottest: the single ingredient people would happily take for free.

That's your hot stepThe one step of your Magic Formula™ that becomes the seed of your Event Magnet™, the free lead magnet you build later.. The Builder flags it here as the seed of your Event Magnet™. You don't build the Event Magnet™ yet.

What makes a strong hot step

  • One clear win. It solves one specific problem from your system.
  • Teachable fast. You could walk someone through it in a short video or PDF.
  • Quick to use. Real value in about 10 minutes, or one sitting.
  • Incomplete by design. It solves one piece and leaves them wanting the full system.
  • An obvious next step. The path to your paid offer is clear once they've used it.

Skip the obvious, foundational steps. Nobody opts in for "Define your avatar." The best hot steps usually come from Phase 1 or early Phase 2, where the first "aha" lives.

Don't confuse the two

Money Magnet™ is a whole pillar, Pillar 2. Event Magnet™ is the one free lead magnet you build from your hot step to feed it.

"Foundation, Framework, Fuel" or "Design, Develop, Deploy."
