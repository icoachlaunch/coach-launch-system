01

A Video on Every Page

Your Cash Flow Engine™ funnel puts a video on its pages. These are the scripts for them.

Each video has one job. The ticket page video sells the ticket. The upgrade page video sells the VIP. The confirmation video tells them what happens next.

The skills write each one word for word, in your voice, from the assets you've already built. Your story, your proof, your tool, your offer.

You get a script you can read straight to camera, with timing markers and notes on what to show.

What it is not

It doesn't write your page copy. That's the Cash Flow Engine™ Funnel Builder.

It doesn't write your emails, your ads or your virtual event presentation. Each of those has its own builder.

And it doesn't film or edit anything. You record what it writes.

The principle

One video, one page, one job. A video that tries to do two things does neither.

03

Two Skills, Every Video

One question decides which scripts you need: how did they get their seat? A free registration, or a paid ticket.

1

The Ticket Sales Detonator™The video that sells the ticket

For a paid ticket page, with a ticket under $100. The viewer is cold, often straight from an ad. They don't know you yet.

Why it matters. This video carries your full story, one client's story, the tool and the case for the day. It's told once, here, so nothing after it has to tell it again.

What you get

A 5–8 minute script: the hook, your story as a scene, the real reason they're stuck, the day and the tool revealed, the picture of life after, then the ticket price, the guarantee and one action.

✕ Not that

"Grab your ticket for my workshop!" It sells a seat on a call.

✓ Do this

Sell what the day starts for them. The ticket is only the door.

The trap: mentioning the VIP. It gets its own video, straight after they buy. This one has one job.

2

The Authority Detonator™Every video after they're in

Everything after someone has their seat. It has two branches, set by how they got in.

Why it matters. A free registrant hasn't heard your story yet, so the video builds it. A ticket buyer heard it a minute ago, so the video moves straight to the next offer.

What you get

The script for one video per run. The two confirmation videos are written as a pair.

✕ Not that

Retelling your whole story on the upgrade page after a paid ticket.

✓ Do this

One line brings it back. Then the offer, and a reason to say yes now.

The trap: teaching the tool in the video. If the video teaches it, there's no reason to turn up.

04

The Hard Rules

These keep your videos selling and keep them working week after week. The skills hold you to every one.

  1. Sell the dream outcome. They're not buying a worksheet or a seat on a call. They're buying what it does for them.
  2. Show the tool, never teach it. Hold it up and point at its pieces. The event does the how.
  3. Nothing is handed over before the day. "This is what you'll get when you show up." Never "it's in your inbox."
  4. Evergreen. No date, time or weekday in the video. The time lives on the page and in the email, so you never re-record.
  5. One big idea, one action. Every video ends on one instruction, pointing at the button under it.
  6. Never pitch your core offer. That's made at the event.
  7. Every paid offer has a guarantee. Your own, or the Coach Launch default. Make sure your checkout terms match it.
  8. Urgency only when it's real. No early-bird pricing, no price that goes up, no fake countdown.
  9. A free event never gives the recording away. Recordings sit in the VIP.
  10. Proof matches what's sold, and is used once. No result appears twice across your funnel's videos.
  11. Spoken, not written. Short sentences, numbers as numerals, in your Brand Voice.

Anything your documents don't hold is flagged in the chat. Nothing is invented to fill the gap.

The downsell rule

The downsell is set by the price of the upsell it follows.

Upsell $100 or moreA payment plan

The same offer, nothing taken out, said as "[N] payments of $[amount]".

Upsell under $100A lower price

With items removed, for example the recordings only. It names exactly what's in and what's out.

05

The Order to Build Them

Each script reads the ones before it, so it never repeats them or contradicts them. Build them in this order.

Your funnelBuild in this order
Free entry, Phase 1The Show-Up video
Free entry, with a VIPThe VIP video → the confirmation pair
Paid ticketThe ticket video → the VIP upsell → its downsell → the PASS™ or second upsell → its downsell, if any → the confirmation pair, last

Approve each script before you start the next one. An approved script is never quietly changed by a later one.

When an asset changes, like your ticket price or your ticket video, re-run the scripts that read it. Never edit a script on its own.

The principle

Your story is told once per funnel. On a paid funnel, the ticket video owns it, and every video after carries it on.

Write Your Scripts

Each skill runs in Claude. You install each one once, then open a new chat for each video.

Skill 1 of 2 · Claude · paid funnels only
AI tool · skill

The Ticket Sales Detonator™

It writes the 5–8 minute video for your paid ticket page. Skip it if your entry is free.

Download the skill (.zip)

Upload the .zip as it is. Don't unzip it: the references inside are what the skill writes from.

  1. Download and install it once. Save cl-ticket-sales-detonator.zip, then in Claude open Settings › Capabilities › Skills, choose Upload skill and upload the .zip. Check: it shows as enabled.
  2. Open a new chat and ask for your ticket video. Start it inside your $100K Day AI Workspace and say "Write my ticket video." Check: it reads your assets and plays back your event, price, tool and story.
  3. Lock the facts. It asks only what your documents can't answer: the honest reason for your price, what the day is worth or replaces, your guarantee, and where each result came from. Never the date.
  4. Approve the brief. The big idea in one line, your two stories, and the target length.
  5. Pick your opening, then approve each section. It gives you at least four openings to choose from, or to split-test. Rewrite any section in your own words and it keeps yours.
  6. Save it. In Client Engine › Money Magnet › cash-flow-engine, as ticket-sales-detonator.md, plus a Google Doc or Word copy to record from.
Skill 2 of 2 · Claude · a new chat per video
AI tool · skill

The Authority Detonator™

It writes every video after someone is in: the Show-Up and VIP videos for a free seat, the upsells and downsells after a paid ticket, and the confirmation videos for both.

Download the skill (.zip)

On a paid funnel, write your ticket video first. This skill reads it and stops without it.

  1. Download and install it once. Save cl-authority-detonator.zip and upload it under Settings › Capabilities › Skills. Check: it shows as enabled.
  2. Open a new chat and name the video. Say "Write my VIP video" or "Write my Show-Up video." Check: it tells you which branch it's on, free or paid, and why.
  3. Lock the facts. It asks only what your documents can't answer: your offer's past price, your guarantee, any trial, whether anything genuinely closes, and your downsell terms.
  4. Approve the brief. The big idea, the target length and why.
  5. Pick your opening, then approve each section. Choose an opening or split-test two or three. Your rewrites are kept word for word.
  6. Save it. In Client Engine › Money Magnet › cash-flow-engine, one file per video (for example authority-detonator-vip.md), plus a Google Doc or Word copy to record from. Then open a new chat for the next video.

Check Your Work

Most weak funnel videos fail in one of these ways. Check yours against each one.

✕ Selling the tool

"You'll get my free worksheet."

✓ Selling the result

Say what the tool gets them, in the words of your promise.

✕ A date in the video

It goes stale next week, and you re-record.

✓ Evergreen

"The time's on this page and in your inbox."

✕ Sending the tool early

"Download it now and bring it along."

✓ Released on the day

That's the reason to be there live.

✕ The full story, twice

The buyer heard it on the ticket page a minute ago.

✓ One line to bring it back

Then straight to the offer.

✕ A fake deadline

"Price goes up at midnight" when it doesn't.

✓ Urgency only when it's real

A real seat cap because it's live, or nothing.

Is it ready?

  • Each video has one job and ends on one action, pointing at the button.
  • No date, time or weekday anywhere in the script.
  • The tool is shown, never taught, and never handed over before the day.
  • Every paid offer states its guarantee, and your checkout terms match it.
  • No result is used twice across your funnel's videos.
  • Your funnel pages match the videos. If they don't, the skill lists the page edits for the Cash Flow Engine™ Funnel Builder.
