01

Your Content Source Code

The SCORE™ Card is the one document every piece of your content is built from.

You've already built the pieces: your message, your avatar, your story, your system. Right now they live in separate places.

The SCORE™ Card pulls them into one master document. Every email, post, video, ad and pitch draws from it.

It's built on the SCORE™ Framework: a five-act arc that moves a reader from a cold scroll to a warm yes, in the order people actually make decisions.

Curiosity, then trust, then desire, then clarity, then commitment. Each act meets the reader exactly where they are.

19

Blocks across 5 acts. Write them once. From then on, every piece of content is an assembly job: pick the blocks that fit the format and arrange them. You never start from a blank page again.

The principle

Build it once. Deploy it everywhere.

What it is not

It's not a piece of content. You don't post your SCORE™ Card. You draw from it.

It's not your offer document. That comes in Step 3, the Red Diamond Offer™, and it's assembled out of these blocks.

It's not written for one format. Every block works in an email, an ad, a video or a stage pitch without a rewrite.

And it's never finished for good. It's a living document that gets stronger every time you land a better result or sharpen your message.

03

How You'll Build It

You don't write nineteen blocksA short piece of core persuasion copy with one job, like the opening question or the story. The card has 19 of them. from scratch. You assemble them from work you've already locked, then sharpen.

The Builder walks it in three moves.

Move 1Extract

It reads your locked work and echoes back your promise, avatar, story and system, so you confirm them before a single block is written.

Move 2Build

One act per turn. It writes every block in that act, in your voice, and pushes back the moment an input is vague.

Move 3Assemble

Once every act passes the Ready Check, it puts the whole card together with notes on which blocks to use for each format.

The four things it asks you for

Almost everything is already in your locked work. These four aren't, so the Builder asks for them in one go.

  1. Credibility anchors. Your real numbers: years doing this, clients served, results delivered. Exact figures only, and always yours.
  2. Client proof. Three to six real results: a name, the specific result with numbers, and the timeline. No client wins yet? Give it your own results and mark them to update later.
  3. Urgency or scarcity. Is anything genuinely limited, like spots in a group, an application or a closing date? If nothing is, say "none."
  4. Result timeline. How fast someone sees a meaningful result once they start.

If your Golden Avatar™ has more than one profile, you build ONE combined card that speaks to what they share. You can build a sharper card per avatar later.

The arc never changes. Only the scale does. A social post squeezes all five acts into a few lines. A sales page expands each one.

05

The Five Acts, In Full

Here's each act: what it does, every block in it, and the trap that quietly weakens it.

S

Serendipity4 blocks · "this is for me"

Serendipity makes your audience feel discovered, not sold to. You interrupt the scroll and replace it with a feeling of recognition.

S-1 · Anchor. Opening copy that builds to your $Million Promise™, word for word, then drives it home. You don't open cold with the promise. You set it up, land it, and reinforce it.

S-2 · Question. One bold, specific question your avatar has quietly been asking themselves. So specific it feels like you read their mind.

S-3 · Symbolism. One vivid metaphor for the problem and the shift. A single image that makes the old way feel obviously wrong.

S-4 · Debunking. The common fix they've already tried that fails, and why. It sets up your system as the real answer without naming it yet.

The strongest openers use the Mirror BlockA move that reflects the reader back to themselves and positions your program as the last stop on their search.: instead of "here's what I do," you say, in effect, "I built this to be the last stop on your journey."

✕ Generic

"Do you want to grow your coaching business and reach more people?"

✓ Specific

"Are you the coach who knows more about marketing than most people in the room, and still can't fill a single event?"

The trap: a question so broad it could open anyone's ad. If it doesn't feel like mind-reading, it's not done.

C

Connection3 blocks + the gap · "this person gets me"

Once you have attention, the guard goes up. Connection dissolves it by proving you understand them. Trust is built here through honesty, not credentials.

C-1 · 3 Problems. The three obstacles keeping your avatar stuck, one for each phase of your Magic Formula™. One focused paragraph each.

C-2 · Story. Your locked $Million Moment™, shaped for the card. The moment everything turned, told so the avatar sees themselves in it.

C-3 · Credibility. Your real numbers and proof. Specific, no approximations, and always your own figures.

The act ends on the Universal GapThe bridge line: they've collected pieces, a tactic here and a strategy there, but never one complete system. Until now.. The problem was never their effort. It was never having the full map. That line hands off straight into your System Reveal.

✕ A résumé

"I've spent 12 years helping founders scale." That's a credential, not a story.

✓ A moment

Where you were, what wasn't working, and the specific decision that built what you teach now.

The trap: turning C-2 into a list of credentials because it feels safer. Save the numbers for C-3.

O

Opportunity3 blocks · "this is what I've been looking for"

Trust is built. The worst move now is switching into hard-sell mode. Instead, you reveal your system as the thing they've been searching for.

O-1 · System Reveal. Your Magic Formula™ in full: the name, all 3 phases and all 9 steps, each with one plain sentence on what it does and why it matters.

O-2 · Differentiator. One clear argument for why this works when everything else didn't. Not a list of features. The one reason.

O-3 · Client Proof. Your strongest 3 to 5 real results, strongest first, each with a name, a result and a timeline. It always ends with the results disclaimer line.

✕ A teaser

Three phases shown, nine steps skipped. It reads as "there's more I'm not telling you."

✓ The complete map

All nine steps, one plain sentence each. The full path is what earns the yes.

The trap: leaving off the disclaimer. "These clients showed up, did the work, and followed the system. Your results depend on your effort and how consistently you implement." Real results come with real conditions.

R

Reality4 blocks · the cost of staying, the life waiting

What moves people most isn't the promise of gain. It's the clarity of what they lose by standing still. Reality paints two honest futures, with no guilt and no hype.

R-1 · Consequence. What your avatar's life looks like 12 months out if nothing changes. Specific and personal: a mirror, not a lecture.

R-2 · Limits. Breaks the "my situation's different" objection by showing the range of situations the system has already worked in.

R-3 · Reward. What life feels like when the system is running. Emotional, forward-looking, specific.

R-4 · Desire. The real want under the stated goal. The thing they haven't said out loud.

✕ A case study

R-3 repeats your client results. Those already live in Act 3.

✓ A feeling

The texture of daily life once it runs: no Sunday dread, no starting from zero each month.

The trap: painting R-1 as a generic warning. If it doesn't feel like their actual week, it won't land.

E

Execution5 blocks · "the next step is obvious"

The decision is forming. Your only job now is to make action the path of least resistance. It's guiding, not pushing.

E-1 · 2 Ways. The two paths, both real: keep the current approach (familiar, not evil) or take this step (different, not perfect).

E-2 · Hard Choice. Names the weight of the decision without preaching. Short and direct.

E-3 · Because. Why now, not later. The specific cost of waiting.

E-4 · FOMO. Genuine scarcity only. If nothing is truly limited, the block gets flagged for you to confirm before you use it.

E-5 · CTA. The one action, with the price and close from your Money Model. Until that's built, E-5 carries a placeholder. This is the only block with offer details in it.

✕ Manufactured

"Only 3 spots left!" when there's no real cap.

✓ Genuine

A real group cap, a real application, a real closing date. Or a flag to confirm.

The trap: fake urgency. Buyers can smell it, and one fake countdown taints the trust you spent four acts building.

06

The Format-Neutral Rule

Every block except E-5 is format-neutralWritten so it works anywhere: an email, a post, a video or a stage pitch. No words that only make sense in one place.: raw persuasion copy with no words tied to one place.

That means no "click the link below," no "as I said on the call," no "in this video." Those get added later, when you assemble a specific piece.

Bake "click below" into a block and it can't go in a video or a stage pitch without a rewrite. Keep the source clean, and every block drops into any format untouched.

E-5 is the one exception. A call to action without the real offer, price and close isn't a call to action.

Which blocks to pull, by format

Before and After

Here's the difference specificity makes: two blocks for a productivity coach who works with overwhelmed founders.

S-2 · Question, weak

"Do you feel busy but unproductive and want to get more done?"

S-2 · Question, built

"Be honest. Are you the founder who works twelve-hour days, ends every one with a longer list than you started with, and quietly wonders if you're just not cut out to run this thing?"

R-4 · Desire, weak

"You want more freedom and less stress in your business."

R-4 · Desire, built

"What you actually want isn't a tidier to-do list. It's to close the laptop at 6pm without the knot in your stomach, to feel like a founder who's running the company, not a person the company is running."

Figure 07 · Same idea, sharper words. An illustrative example, made up to show the shape. No real client or result is implied.

The weak versions name a category. The built versions name the exact feeling they've never said out loud.

Nothing about the framework changed. Everything about the specificity did. That's the Builder's whole job: it won't let a vague input become a vague block.

08

The Hard Rules

These keep your SCORE™ Card honest and reusable. The Builder holds you to every one.

  1. Assemble from what's locked. The card is built from your $Million Story™ and Magic Formula™. You shape proven material, not new claims.
  2. Never fabricate. No invented results, testimonials or numbers. If something's missing, it's flagged inside the block, never filled with a guess.
  3. Your words stay your words. Names, prices and phrases are used exactly. Weak language gets flagged, never quietly rewritten.
  4. All nineteen get built. You can choose not to use a block you have. You can't use a block you don't.
  5. Specific or it doesn't ship. Vague in, vague out. Every block earns its place by being specific to your avatar.
  6. Format-neutral, except E-5. E-5 alone carries your offer, price and close.
  7. Your numbers, not Matthew's. His figures are his credentials. Your credibility block uses your own real figures.

How It's Checked

The SCORE™ Card isn't scored out of 25, and that's on purpose.

The assets it's built from were already scored and locked. These nineteen blocks are creative copy assembled from that work, so the test is a pass-or-fail Ready CheckA pass-or-fail check, act by act. Every item has to pass before the card is assembled., act by act.

ActIt passes when
SerendipityS-1 holds your $Million Promise™ word for word and builds to it. The question, metaphor and debunk are specific to your avatar.
ConnectionThree distinct problems, one per phase. C-2 is a real story, not a résumé. C-3 uses your own numbers. The Universal Gap bridges into Opportunity.
OpportunityO-1 shows all 3 phases and all 9 steps. O-2 makes one clear argument. O-3 uses confirmed results and ends with the disclaimer.
RealityR-1 is a personal mirror. R-3 is the feeling of the life, not repeated results. R-4 names the real want.
ExecutionE-4 uses genuine scarcity, or is flagged. E-5 carries your price and close, or a clear placeholder until the Money Model is built.

If a block fails, the Builder names the gap and fixes that block. It never forces a pass.

Three phases, but the nine steps squeezed into one sentence.

✓ The full Magic Formula™

All nine steps, one plain sentence each.

"Click the button below" living inside a block.
