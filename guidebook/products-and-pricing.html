01

What You Sell, On One Page

Your Offer Pricing doc is the plain record of what you sell, laid out as three levels a client can climb, each one named and priced by you.

Most coaches start with one offer and a price they're not quite sure about.

That's fine as a start. It's a problem as a business, because one offer means every month starts from zero. A client buys once, gets a result and leaves, and you go and find the next one.

This document fixes the shape. A membership at the bottom, a faster option in the middle, and closer access at the top. Each one a step up in price, and a step closer to you.

That's the ascension modelA client starts small, gets a win and climbs to the next level. The people who finish the upper levels fall back into the membership instead of leaving..

3

Three levels. One ladder. Every price in one place. Your Money Model, your Enrollment Doc, your funnel pages and your presentation all read what you sell from this one document.

It matters because every tool after this needs to know your prices, and none of them should have to ask you.

Write them down once, here, in the shape the whole system understands, and the AI stops guessing what you charge.

What it is not

It's not your Money Model. That's a Step 3 asset: it picks your Accelerator phase, chooses your core offer, runs the maths and locks the price.

It's not your offer. The value stack, bonuses, guarantee and sales copy live in your Red Diamond Offer™ Enrollment Doc. This is the list of what exists, not the pitch for it.

It's not a pricing strategy session. The Builder tells you where prices usually land. It never sets a number for you, and never moves one you've chosen.

And it's not a business plan. Twenty minutes, thirteen questions, one document.

The principle

One offer is a job. Three levels is a business.

04

The Three Levels, In Full

Three levels, three fixed forms. You name each one and set each price. The forms don't change, because every tool after this one reads the document knowing exactly what a level means.

Level 1Confidence

Your membership. Usually around $5,000 a year, with the monthly worked back from it.

Level 2Acceleration

Done-for-you, or a 3-day accelerator. Usually $10,000 to $15,000 and up.

Level 3Proximity

1-on-1, a retreat or a mastermind. Usually $20,000 and up, some to $100,000.

Figure 04 · The three levels. The bands show where prices usually land in this system. They're a guide, not a rule, and the number you record is yours.

1

ConfidenceYour membership

The foundation of your training and coaching: your trainings, your system, your community, and a lower level of coaching, most often a weekly group call.

It's where a client gets confident the system works, and confident working with you.

Why it matters. It's the floor of the whole business. It fills the room at your events, it's what a new client can say yes to, and it's what the upper levels fall back into when their term ends.

How to shape yours

Keep it to training, community and group coaching. Enough for a result, light enough that it doesn't compete with the levels above it.

Price it annual first. The year is the price you lead with. The monthly exists for people who can't pay for the year, and you always show both.

✕ Not that

A library of recordings with no calls and no community. That's a course. Nobody falls back into a course.

✓ Do this

A training library, a weekly group coaching call and a private community, at [annual price] a year, or [monthly price] a month for those who need it.

The trap: putting everything in it. Add 1-on-1 access and done-for-you pieces, and you've priced Level 3 work at Level 1. There's nothing left to climb to.

2

AccelerationFaster

One of two forms. Done-for-you, where you or your team do the work for them. Or a 3-day accelerator, a bootcamp-style event where they build it with you in three days.

Either way the value is speed: the same outcome, sooner.

Why it matters. It's the middle of the ladder, and it's where a lot of your cash comes from. It also gives the membership somewhere to send people who want to move faster than a weekly call allows.

How to shape yours

Pick the form honestly. If you and your team deliver, it's done-for-you. If you'd rather have a room of people building together, it's the 3-day accelerator.

Give it a term: three months, three days plus ninety days of support, six months. And decide the fallback: when it ends, they continue in your membership.

✕ Not that

"The membership, but with two calls a week." That's more of Level 1, not a faster route to the result.

✓ Do this

"A 3-day build, then ninety days of weekly check-ins, then they roll into the membership." A term, a fallback, a clear reason it's faster.

The trap: an accelerator with no end. If it never ends, it's a membership with a bigger price, and there's no moment where the client falls back into the real one.

3

ProximityCloser

One of three forms: 1-on-1, a retreat, or a mastermind. Direct access to you, and to a small group already getting the result. The top of the ladder.

Why it matters. It's where your best clients want to be, and it's priced like it. Because it's your time, it's capped, and the cap is part of what makes it worth the price.

How to shape yours

Pick the form, set the number of seats you can genuinely hold, give it a term, and decide the fallback: the membership, same as Level 2.

✕ Not that

"Unlimited 1-on-1, as many as sign up." Proximity means your time. Without a cap it isn't proximity, and it isn't sustainable.

✓ Do this

"A mastermind, eight seats, twelve months, two in-person days a quarter and a monthly call with me, then the membership."

The trap: fixed deliverables with no access. If the client gets a set of outputs and never gets near you, that's Level 2 wearing a Level 3 price tag.

The fallback: what makes the ladder grow

The membership is the foundation, and both upper levels fall back onto it.

Your accelerator runs for its term. When it ends, the client's next step is the membership. Same for the mastermind or the 1-on-1 work when its year is up.

So a big sale doesn't end when the programme does. It turns into recurring revenue, and the business grows on top of itself instead of starting from zero every month.

The Builder records this as one line on each upper level: Then: continues in [your membership]. Want a different fallbackWhere a client goes when an upper level ends. In this system it's the membership, unless you choose otherwise.? You can have it. The Builder flags it once and records yours.

05

The Annual-First Maths

Your membership has two prices, always shown together. The year is the one you lead with, and it carries a saving on twelve months of the monthly.

25% is the standard. You can choose your own saving, and the Builder records it and uses it in the maths. What matters is that the year is clearly the better deal.

Annual = Monthly × 12 × (1 − saving)
Monthly = Annual ÷ (12 × (1 − saving))

Start from whichever number you hold. Know what the year should cost? The Builder works the monthly back. Only ever thought in monthly? It works the year forward.

You start withAt the standard 25%Both prices shown
$5,000 a year$5,000 ÷ (12 × 0.75)$5,000 a year, or about $556 a month
$6,000 a year$6,000 ÷ (12 × 0.75)$6,000 a year, or about $667 a month
$500 a month$500 × 12 × 0.75$4,500 a year, or $500 a month

Figure 05 · The maths, worked three ways. Illustrative numbers to show the sum, not prices to copy.

Round either number to something clean if you like. The Builder will offer, and the decision is yours.

Why annual first

Lead with the monthly and you've built a subscription people cancel. Lead with the year and you've built a high-ticket offer paid over time.

Your Shelf and Your Goal

What you sell today: the map

Before the Builder teaches you the levels, it asks for everything already on your shelf. Then it places each thing.

If it's…It sits at
A recurring offer with ongoing coaching and communityLevel 1 · Confidence
A fixed-term offer where speed is the value: done-for-you, or a multi-day buildLevel 2 · Acceleration

Anything under Other is a front-end product, not a level. The Builder never invents a fourth rung to hold it.

Then the gaps show. Whatever level has nothing on it is the one you shape next and mark Planned. Most coaches find they already have one level without knowing it, and the other two are the plan.

Your revenue goal

Your Business Brand Profile keeps the picture of where you're headed. This document keeps the number.

Give it in whatever shape you hold it: monthly recurring, a yearly total, or a number of clients. And give it a date. A goal with a date is a plan. A goal without one is a wish.

If it's a number pulled from the air, say so. The Builder keeps it, marks it UNVERIFIED, and your Money Model will show you what it takes to get there.

08

The Hard Rules

These keep your ladder honest and readable by every tool after it. The Builder holds you to every one.

  1. Level 1 is always a membership. Training, community and group coaching, paid annual or monthly. Everything else falls back into it.
  2. Annual first, then monthly. You lead with the year, and you show both every time.
  3. The saving is yours to set. 25% is the standard. The Builder records what you choose and uses it in the maths.
  4. The forms are fixed. Level 2 is done-for-you or a 3-day accelerator. Level 3 is 1-on-1, a retreat or a mastermind. The names and prices are yours.
  5. Every upper level has a term and a fallback. It ends, and the client continues in your membership.
  6. No fourth rung. Whatever fits none of the three forms sits under Other, below the ladder.
  7. A low price gets flagged once, never moved. The number you record is yours.
  8. Price points here, the price locked in the Money Model.
  9. Nothing from another document. No phase, no close, no projections, no offer copy, no avatar, no competitors.
  10. A marked unknown is fine. Planned, TBD and UNVERIFIED are all real answers, and all pass.

✓ Training, coaching and community

Add the weekly group call and the community. That makes it a membership.

✕ Everything in the membership

1-on-1, done-for-you and unlimited calls at the lowest price on the ladder. Nothing left to climb to.

✓ Level 1 kept lean

Closer access is what people climb for, and it's priced that way.

✕ Leading with the monthly

A monthly price on the page, the yearly buried. That's a subscription people cancel in month two.

✓ Annual first, every time

The saving clear, the monthly beside it for those who genuinely can't pay for the year.

✕ An accelerator that never ends

"Ongoing done-for-you support" with no term. No fallback, so the one sale is the whole relationship.

✓ A term and a fallback
