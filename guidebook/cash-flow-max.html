01

Cash on the Way In

Cash Flow Max™ builds the paid add-ons inside your event: your VIP upsell and your order bumps.

Your free event fills the room. Your main offer gets closed at the event. But right in between, at the checkout, there's money most coaches leave on the table.

A VIP upgrade and a couple of small bumps turn a free or low-cost registration into instant cash.

That cash pays for your ads as you go. It's how you fill the room without going broke, which is the whole point of your Cash Flow Engine™.

Cash Flow Max™ = a VIP upsell + order bumps, all pulled from your system

Figure 01 · The shape of Cash Flow Max™. Two kinds of add-on, one cart.

Matthew White built Coach Launch on offers shaped like this: $24.6 Million in high-ticket sales generated and $5.2 Million in recurring client results delivered, where the cash on the way in paid for the traffic that filled the room.

The principle

Real value AND a bigger cart. Both, or neither. Every add-on comes from your Magic Formula™, genuinely helps the buyer, and carries its own promise.

How it's different from the Event Magnet™

They sit at opposite ends of the same event. The Event Magnet™ is FREE, and it gets people into the room. Cash Flow Max™ is the paid end: the upgrade they choose at checkout and the bumps they tick on the order form.

What it is not

It's not your main offer. That's your Red Diamond Offer™, closed at the event with The Sniper Close™.

It's not a pile of random freebies stacked up to look valuable. And it's not a discount play. You add value to raise the cart, you don't slash prices.

05

The VIP: Sell Access

Your VIP is where most of this cash comes from. And most people build it backwards.

They lead with freebies. But people upgrade to get closer to YOU. So you lead with the live time, then stack real value around it.

1

The RecordingsCore 1 · every VIP has it

Lifetime or extended access to the event replays, so they can go back to the breakthroughs while they build.

Why it matters. It costs you almost nothing and it's worth a lot to them. A recording comes only with the VIP.

2

The VIP Q&ACore 2 · the heart of the VIP

Reserved VIP seats, plus dedicated Q&A or extra time with you, where THEIR questions about THEIR business get answered.

Why it matters. This is the real reason people upgrade. Put it first.

✕ Not that

A VIP headline about the workbook and the replays. Files feel skippable.

✓ Do this

Lead with the live time with you. Stack the extras around it as support.

The trap: thinking "more stuff" is the value. People don't upgrade for files. They upgrade for access.

Then stack it

The standard stacker is community: VIP status in a group or inner circle where they build rapport and stay connected after the event. The people in your community are the people who buy again.

Optional stackers, only if they're real: a day-after deep-dive session, a workbook or template pack, or a fast-start bonus training. Never invent value to pad the stack.

Single VIP or double VIP

Single VIPOne upgrade: the recordings, the VIP Q&A, community and any real stackers, anchored high to lowExecute and Growth

The magic words on a double VIP are "everything in A, plus…" Option B never starts from scratch. It climbs.

06

The Bumps, and a Promise on Every Piece

Bumps are the impulse yeses: small tick-box add-ons on your order form. A great bump is an obvious "well, of course," one small thing that makes the ticket work better.

✕ Not a bump

A full course, a big program, a guide or ebook, a live call, or anything that takes weeks to build or hours to use.

✓ A bump

A template or swipe file, a cheat sheet, checklist or scorecard, one short focused training, or a calculator or planner used in one sitting.

$7–$47

The bump price band. Bumps can go higher, but that's rarely right. Never go below $7, because the friction isn't worth it.

Every piece gets its own $Million Promise™

This is the move that separates a cart that sells from one that just sits there. A list of parts is a cost. A named upgrade that promises a result is a deal.

So the VIP, and every single bump, gets its own promise: name the thing, promise a specific result, and put a clock on it.

[Asset name] — [specific result] in [timeline], without [obstacle]

Figure 06 · The promise on every piece. The same shape as your $Million Promise™, sized for one add-on.

✕ Just a thing with a price

"VIP access — $97." "Bonus templates — $27." No result, no reason to tick the box.

✓ Named with a promise

"The 7-Touch Bump — book 5 calls this week, without cold outreach."

Name it first. The price always looks better after the promise.

07

Real Value, Honest Numbers

Here's the line you don't cross: every add-on has to genuinely help the buyer get the result faster or better.

The VIP's extra value must be REAL extra value: more access, more depth, more speed. If a piece is just filler, cut it. A shorter, honest stack beats a padded one.

Anchor the value, don't fake it

Show each piece's normal value next to its low add-on price, so the deal is obvious. That's honest anchoring.

But the value has to be a real price for that piece. A fake "$5,000 value" on a one-page checklist sells once, then gets refunded and remembered.

Don't let the cart compete with the offer

The bumps make the ticket better. The VIP makes the day better. Neither replaces your Red Diamond Offer™.

If an add-on starts to feel like the main event, pull it back, so the cart feeds the close instead of competing with it.

09

The Hard Rules and the Check

These keep Cash Flow Max™ raising cart value the right way. The Builder holds you to every one.

  1. Everything comes from your Magic Formula™. Every VIP piece and every bump is on-system.
  2. Every piece gets its own $Million Promise™. Name it, promise a result, put a clock on it.
  3. Structure follows the phase. Change it only with a reason.
  4. Real value, not junk. No filler to raise the total.
  5. Easy to build, easy to use. Bumps are one-sitting tools.
  6. Anchor honestly. Bumps $7–$47, every value figure real.
  7. Don't compete with the offer. The cart warms the buyer for your Red Diamond Offer™.
  8. Your numbers, not Matthew's. Never borrow numbers or invent results.

The Ready Check

Cash Flow Max™ isn't scored out of 25. The Magic Formula™ and the $Million Promise™ it's built from were already scored. So this is pass/fail: seven checks, and all seven have to pass.
