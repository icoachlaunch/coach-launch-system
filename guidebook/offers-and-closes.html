01

The Offer Ladder

You sell three offers, and each phase reaches one rung higher.

Rung 1 · Phases 1–2Membership Offer

$3K–$5K+ a year, paid annually: 20%–25% off the monthly subscription. Ongoing access. The easiest first yes, and the door to everything above it.

Rung 2 · Phase 3Accelerator Offer

$10K–$15K+. Done-for-you, or a bootcamp or workshop-style environment.

Rung 3 · Phase 4Exclusivity Offer

$25K+. Proximity: a retreat, a mastermind, personal one-to-one or in person. The highest value.

The offers aren't separate businesses. A member who joins in Phase 2 is offered your Accelerator from the enrollment call. By Phase 4, your Exclusivity Offer sits at the top with your Accelerator Offer alongside it.

You build your own offers in Step 3: your Money Model sets the prices, and your Red Diamond Offer™ makes them impossible to refuse.

The principle

The membership isn't a consolation prize. It's the first yes, and every rung above it is sold to people who already said yes once.

03

Where the Conversation Sits

The closes don't differ by how much money changes hands. They differ by where the conversation sits next to the sale.

ASK™
On the call. The sale happens there.
JOIN™
After the sale. They buy, then you talk on the enrollment call.
PASS™
Before the sale. You talk in the session, then they buy.
SEAL™
After the deposit, on the onboarding call. The balance is paid before it starts.

And every one of them ends on a call, so nobody finishes without a next step.
