The Numbers

Pick your phase, put in your numbers, and see what one 28-day cycle should bring in, and whether your cart pays for your ads.

01

How to Use It

  1. Pick your phase. The fields change to match that phase's funnel and close.
  2. Put in your ad spend and cost per registration. These two start empty. They're yours, from your ads manager.
  3. Check the rest. Prices start at the low end of your phase's range. Rates start at the framework's starting targets, or the benchmark to move up. Change any of them to your own.
  4. Read the results. Then change one number at a time and watch what moves.

Your numbers stay in this browser, so you can come back and update them after every cycle.

Reading the Results

Registrations
Your ad spend divided by your cost per registration, across the whole cycle.
Cart money in
Everything paid before the event: ticket, VIP, bumps, the PASS™ upsell and Upsell 2, whichever your phase uses.
Ad spend paid back
Cart money in as a share of your ad spend. Track it from Phase 2, aim for 50% or more in Phase 3, and 100% in Phase 4, when the cart pays for the ads.
New clients
The people who buy your phase's offer through its close.
Revenue this cycle
Cart money in, plus PASS™ Sessions booked from the room, plus your offer sales.
These are estimates, not promises

The starting targets are set to be reachable, not to impress. Replace every one with your own numbers as soon as you have them. Your real numbers always beat the defaults.
