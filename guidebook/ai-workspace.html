01

One Folder That Knows You

Your AI Workspace is the single source of truth about your business, built for AI to read.

It's a folder you set up inside Claude, once.

Inside it lives everything the AI needs to know about you: who you help, how you sound, how you look, what you sell, and the system you run.

Once it's set up, you never have to re-explain your business again.

Every time you build something, like an email, a page, a pitch or an ad, the AI already knows your voice, your offer and your frameworks. It builds like you, not like a generic robot.

This is the very first thing you do. Before the Foundations, before the Engine, before anything.

The principle

Set it up once and it works everywhere. It's the ground the whole system stands on.

02

You Don't Have a Marketing Problem

Here's the truth most people miss. You don't have a marketing problem. You don't even have an AI problem.

You have a systems problem.

Think about how you use AI right now. You open a chat, explain your business, get something decent and close the tab.

Tomorrow you open a new chat and explain your business all over again. Different tool, same re-explaining, every single time.

The AI never remembers you. So every output starts from zero, and every output sounds a little generic.

The fix isn't a better prompt. It's a system: one place the AI reads first, every time.

The layer most people miss

Most people only ever use the first three layers of AI. All three share the same weakness: the AI doesn't know your business.

LayerWhat it doesThe catch
1 · ChatYou ask, it answersIt forgets you the moment you close the tab
2 · CreationIt writes and makes things for youIt still starts from nothing each time
3 · AgentsIt takes actions and runs tasksPointed at the wrong context, it does the wrong thing faster
4 · SystemsOne documented system carries your voice, offer, frameworks and numbers into every sessionNone. This is what your AI Workspace is

Build the Systems Layer once, and every layer above it gets sharper.

05

How It Remembers

Your workspace remembers everything you build, and no single file ever gets heavy. It does that with three layers, each with one job.

LayerWhat it holdsWhen Claude reads it
CONTEXT.mdThe index. One line per fact: what's built, what's next, how you like to work.First, every session
NOTES.mdThe memory beside each asset: the decisions and why, what you turned down, what's still open.Only when it's working on that asset
The asset fileThe finished thing, with its version history at the topWhen a builder or skill needs it

Your five Foundations share one notes file, Reference/NOTES.md, with a heading for each document.

You don't maintain any of this. Claude writes it as you build. You just get an AI that already knows.

06

Where the Builders Live

The builders are not in the kit. That's on purpose.

A builderA guided AI interview that builds one document or asset with you, one question at a time. is a prompt you paste into a chat. They live in Coach Launch Academy™, your Training Portal, where they get improved over time. Each chapter of this Guidebook carries the builder it teaches.

When Claude says it's time to build something, you get that builder, paste it into the chat and answer one question at a time. Claude saves the result into the right folder.

So your folders never go stale when a builder improves.

The principle

The workspace holds your business. The builders hold the method. Keep them apart and both stay current.

07

Your Five Foundations

These are the five documents you build first. Each has its own builder: a short, guided interview, one question at a time. Each one saves over its empty starter file in Reference/.

  1. Business Brand Profile. What you believe, why you exist, where you're headed, where you operate.
  2. Visual Style Guide. Your look. You pick one Brand Kit and install it in Reference/brand-kit/.
  3. Brand Voice. How you write and speak, so the AI sounds like you.
  4. Products & Pricing. What you sell, how you price it, how you deliver it.
  5. Competitive Landscape. Your market, your competitors and your edge.

Do them in order. They take about an hour for all five, and each one makes the next builder sharper.

A starter file says status: empty at the top. A finished one says status: complete.

One Brand Kit, installed once

Your look isn't a list of colours you try to remember. It's a real file.

In your Visual Style Guide you pick one of six Kits and save its file into Reference/brand-kit/tokens/. Every slide, page, ad and post reads from it.

That folder holds exactly one Kit. You can change it later, but you replace it. Two Kits is two brands, and every tool has to guess which one is true.

10

The Rules That Keep It Clean

As your workspace grows, a few rules keep it from turning into a mess. Claude follows every one.

  1. One file per asset. You update the same file over time. Never a "-v2" or a "-FINAL". Claude bumps the version and keeps a little history right inside the file.
  2. One Brand Kit at a time. When you change it, the old one goes. Replace, never add.
  3. Reference is the truth. Client Engine™ is what you build from it. Facts about your business, like your team or your guarantee, go in Reference/. Things you build, like a podcast or a workshop outline, go in Client Engine/.
  4. Your own assets get their own folder. In the pillar they serve, named in lowercase with hyphens, like podcast/. Never inside another asset's folder, and never loose in a pillar folder.
  5. Three pillars hold everything. Shaping the offer, making the sale or finding people. There is never a fourth.
  6. The builders come from Coach Launch Academy™. Get the current version there each time, so you always run the latest.

That's it. One promise, one profile, one voice, one Kit, each living in one place and always current.
