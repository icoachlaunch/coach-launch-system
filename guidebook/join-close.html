01

When You Use It

The JOIN™ Close is the Phase 2 close. You use it when your message is proven and you're ready to make your offer from the room.

Phase 2 is Execute, the Offer Validator. Your event is a single day, 90–120 minutes, every week. Entry is free or a $27 ticket, with a VIP from $47, aiming for $97.

What it sells is your Membership Offer: $3K–$5K+ a year, paid annually, which is 20%–25% off the monthly subscription. It's ongoing access, the easiest first yes, and the door to everything above it.

The sale is only the start. Next comes the enrollment call, and from there the upsell to your Accelerator Offer ($10K–$15K+).

You're ready
You've graduated Phase 1. Across one 28-day cycle, your landing page converts at 20% or more and you've collected 50–100 survey responses. Your message works.
Not yet
Your landing page converts under 20%, or you have fewer than 50 survey responses. Stay in Phase 1 and keep running The ASK™ Close. A bigger close won't fix a message that doesn't land yet.
Also not yet
Your membership has no name, no price from your Money Model, or no rhythm for members to show up to. Build those first.
Still available
The ASK™ Close. You can still invite people to apply for a call alongside the room offer.
Later on
The JOIN™ Close stays available in Growth and Mastery.

02

Why It Works

The JOIN™ Close sits after the sale. They buy in the room, then you talk.

The Four Closes don't differ by how much money changes hands. They differ by where the conversation sits next to the sale.

With The ASK™ Close, the sale happens on the call. With The PASS™ Close, you talk in the session first, then they buy. The JOIN™ Close flips it. The room says yes first, and the enrollment call comes after.

That works because of what you're selling. The membership is the easiest first yes. It doesn't need a long one-to-one conversation to decide. It needs a room that has spent time with you.

The law it rides

Immersion = Conversion. The longer people spend with you, the easier the close. Your Phase 2 event runs 90–120 minutes, up from 60–90 in Phase 1. That extra time is what lets you make the offer from the room for the first time.

The second law, people who pay, pay attention, starts here too. VIP is your first money-in. But the close itself rides on immersion.

Why it ends on a call

A membership doesn't die from bad closing. It dies at the second payment.

Someone joins and feels good about it. Then they get busy, never log in, see a second payment leave their account, and cancel. You didn't lose them in the room. You lost them in the fortnight afterwards.

So The JOIN™ Close doesn't end when they say yes. It ends when they've started.

The principle

The membership isn't a consolation prize. It's a front door, and every rung above it is sold to people who already said yes once.

The first month is where they do the work. Your job is to get them past month two, where memberships are lost.

Who does it. They do the work. You keep the rhythm: a live call, a thread, a deadline.

What makes it work. Three things keep a member. A win in the first fortnight. A face they know, with one conversation with you and one with another member. And a reason to show up next week. A membership without rhythm is just a folder of videos.

What makes it work. The order. The upsell comes from the enrollment call, once they've started, not before. The bigger decision lands better with someone who already said yes once and has seen it work.

04

The Ask

Your Sniper Presentation™ has done its work. You've made the offer, walked the value stack and revealed the price. Now the ask, in four beats.

  1. Name what they're joining. Not "my membership." Say its name and what being in it means. People aren't buying access to content. They're buying a room, a rhythm, and you.
  2. Give two ways to pay, and make the annual obviously better. Your Membership Offer is priced by the year, paid annually at 20%–25% off the monthly subscription. Show the monthly beside it so the saving is plain. Then let the saving persuade. Don't push.
  3. Say what happens next. "When you join, you'll get a call from me in the next few days to get you set up." Say it in the ask, not after. They're not buying a login, they're buying a start.
  4. Give the steps, then stop talking. Numbered, on screen. Pick monthly or annual, join, done. Don't re-explain the prices. Don't justify. Let it sit.
How it sounds

"So here's what I'd like to do. I'd like you inside [membership name]. That's where we do this together, every month, with the people already doing it."

"Two ways in. You can join monthly, or you can take the annual and save 25%. Same thing, you just pay once and save yourself a chunk."

"And when you join, you'll get a call from me in the next few days to get you set up properly. You're not just getting a login."

"Link's on the screen. Pick the one that suits you. I'll see you on the inside."

Why two prices

The monthly gets the yes. It's a smaller number, so it's the easiest yes in the room.

The annual keeps the member. There's no second payment to reconsider and no month-two wobble. They're in for the year, which gives them time to get a result. And they hand you the cash up front, which pays for the next event.

The saving is the lever, and 20%–25% is the shape that works. Too small and nobody switches. Too big and you've discounted your own offer for no reason.

Your actual prices come from your Money Model. Don't invent them on stage, and don't discount in the room because it went quiet. The annual saving is your discount. It's already built in.

05

The Call

The enrollment call is the O in JOIN™. The sale already happened. Its first job is to get them started.

Book it within a few days of them joining. Keep it to twenty or thirty minutes.

  1. Welcome and reassure. Tell them they made a good call, and mean it. A little buyer's remorse is normal. This is where you settle it.
  2. Find out where they are. One question: what made you join, and what do you want in the next 90 days? You need it in their words, because it tells you where to point them first.
  3. Point at ONE thing. Not the whole library. One first action, chosen for them from what they just told you. Overwhelm is what makes members go quiet.
  4. Get them into the room. Make sure they're in the community and have posted or said hello once while you're with them. Don't leave it to them.
  5. Book the next touchpoint. The next live call or session, whatever your rhythm is. Put it in their diary before you hang up. Never end the call without a specific next date.

It isn't a coaching session. You're orienting, not solving. Twenty minutes, one direction, done.

If a lot of people join, shorten the calls to fifteen minutes or run them in small groups. Don't replace them with an email.

And don't lead the call with the next offer. Get them started first. The upsell to your Accelerator Offer comes from there.

  • Your rhythm: the next live call or session you'll book them onto.

Common Mistakes

✕ Not that

Presenting the membership as the cheap option, for people who can't afford the real offer. The room hears exactly that.

✓ Do this

Present it as the front door it is. It's a serious commitment, priced by the year.

✕ Not that

Skipping the enrollment calls when a lot of people join, and sending a welcome email instead.

✓ Do this

Run every call. Shorten them to fifteen minutes or run small groups if you must, but never swap them for an email.

✕ Not that

Pitching your Accelerator Offer the moment they've paid. Now the membership feels like a tripwire.

✓ Do this

Get them started first. The next level is sold from results, to someone who has already seen it work.

✕ Not that

"Here's the portal, there are 60 modules, dive in." They feel behind before they've begun.

✓ Do this

One thing. Chosen by you, for them, from what they said they wanted.

✕ Not that

Showing only the monthly price. Every member faces a fresh decision every 30 days.

✓ Do this

Show the annual with its real saving. The people who were serious will take it, and they're the ones who stay.

09

Is It Ready?

No score here. You either have the pieces or you don't.

  • Earned. You've hit the Phase 1 benchmark across one full 28-day cycle.
  • Named. Your membership has a name, and you can say what being in it means.
  • Priced. The annual and the monthly, with a real saving, both from your Money Model.
  • Promised. The enrollment call is named in the ask, not added later.
  • Bookable. Every new member can get onto a call within days.
  • Pointed. You know the ONE thing a new member should do first.
  • Rhythmic. There's a next touchpoint to book them onto.
  • Measured. You're tracking who's still there at payment three.

All eight? Your JOIN™ Close is ready, and the people who say yes will still be with you in three months.
