01

Why it works

Think about the difference between being told how to read a map and being handed one with your own street on it. The first is a lesson. The second is something you use, and the moment you use it, you start looking at where else it could take you.

Most presentations are lessons. People listen, they take notes, they say thank you, and they leave with nothing in their hands.

An Event Magnet™ changes what kind of event you're running. The room isn't watching you explain your Magic Formula™. They're using a piece of it, with you, and getting something finished. That's their first wave from Chapter 4.

And because it's one page that solves one problem, it leaves the next problem in plain sight. They've got the tool, they can see it works, and they can see how much is still missing.

Look at what it does and doesn't give you. It gives you the order of a presentation that closes, and your close for your next event. It doesn't give you the words that go in each box. That's the question it leaves you holding, and it's meant to.

The six checks

Every Event Magnet™ passes the six checks from Chapter 4: it's on your Magic Formula™, it's visual, it's a tool and not a lecture, it gives a quick win, it's incomplete by design, and it leads to one next step.

What it leaves unsolved

This is the part most people get wrong, so it's worth saying plainly. Your Event Magnet™ is supposed to leave something unsolved. It solves one problem completely and honestly, and in doing that it shows them the next one.

Three things get written down for every Event Magnet™:

  • What it deliberately doesn't solve.
  • The question it leaves them holding. For the Sniper Close Sequence: "So I know the order… but what do I actually say at each step?"
  • The bridge line that opens your full Magic Formula™: "That's one step of nine. Here are the other eight."

Where yours comes from

Your Event Magnet™ is built from one step of your Magic Formula™, with its own builder, and that builder is part of the full system. This training shows you what a finished one looks like and how to use it in your presentation, so you know exactly what you're aiming at.

03

Engaging the room

A tool gives you the best engagement of the whole event, because the room has something to do and something to report back.

  • When you first show it: "Who wants this? Give me a YES in the chat."
  • The do-it-now: one instruction they can follow on the spot. For the Sniper Close Sequence: "Circle the event you're running next, then put your finger on the close that sits with it."
  • The report back: "Type your close in the chat: ASK, JOIN, PASS or SEAL."
  • The catch-up line, for anyone who hasn't got it open: one question that gets them the same answer without the page.
  • When you hand it over: "Type GOT IT when it's in your hands."

05

The steps

  1. Look at the Sniper Close Sequence and find each of the seven parts on the page.
  2. Name the step of your Magic Formula™ your presentation teaches. This is the piece from your Chapter 4 action.
  3. Write the win. What would someone have finished after one sitting with a tool for that step?
  4. Write what it wouldn't solve, and the question that would leave them holding.
  5. Write your do-it-now. One instruction, and the word you'd ask them to type back.

06

Mistakes to avoid

  • Treating it as a freebie. It isn't a gift for turning up. It's the centre of your presentation.
  • Making it a teach. If they read it and don't do anything with it, it's a handout.
  • Covering too much. One step. More than one and there's no gap left for your offer to fill.
  • Handing it over early. It's your open loop. It goes into their hands at the end.
  • Hiding the bigger system. "You are here" is what turns a nice tool into the first piece of something they want all of.

07

Check your understanding

  1. What is an Event Magnet™? A one-page tool built from one step of your Magic Formula™, used in the room, that gives a real win in one sitting.

  3. Why is it incomplete by design? Because solving one problem properly shows them the next one, and the next one is what your offer solves.
