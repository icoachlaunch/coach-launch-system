Why

Why You Build Moulds First

Think about a baker with a set of tins. Every loaf comes out the same shape, however many they bake, and nobody has to think about the shape again.

Moulds do that for your slides. You decide your look once. After that, every deck you make comes out in the same shapes and the same colours, and all you ever change is the words.

Layer 1Your colours and fonts

One short block at the top of the file. It's the only place a colour is written, so changing it changes every slide.

Layer 2Two surfaces

Light and dark. Most of your deck sits on one. Your stories and your price sit on the other, so they stand out.

Layer 3Thirteen moulds

One shape for each job a slide does in your presentation.

Every slide also carries a strip along the bottom with the question you ask the room, so you never forget to ask it.
