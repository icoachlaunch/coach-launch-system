01

Why it works

By now they trust you. The temptation is to reward that trust by teaching everything you know, and that's exactly how good presenters end up with a grateful room and no sales.

Phase 2 does something more useful. It takes one piece of your Magic Formula™ and lets them feel the difference it makes, three times over, from three angles. Each time they see their problem as it is now, then they see it solved.

That contrast is what creates desire. People don't want a method. They want the life on the other side of the problem, and Phase 2 shows it to them one pain at a time.

The master shape: Hell → why it exists → Heaven

Every section of your teach uses this shape, and you'll use it again in Phase 3.

  1. Hell. Their pain as a scene: a Tuesday morning, a screen, a conversation, in their words.
  2. The teaching point. "That's exactly why [this part of your Event Magnet™] exists." What it is and why it matters. One idea.
  3. Heaven. What it feels like solved, as an image or an analogy they can see.

Two rules keep it sharp. Hell is a scene, never a category: "they struggle with sales" is a category. Heaven is a picture, never an adjective: "freedom" and "peace of mind" on their own are adjectives.

The words

  • The Big Shift: "The reason [problem] keeps happening isn't [what they think]. It's [your insight]."
  • What you'll hold: "When you've filled this in, you'll have [the finished thing]. For the first time, you can [X] without [Y]."
  • The Confidence Check: "Drop a YES if you can see how this changes [their outcome]."

03

Engaging the room

Teaching is where rooms go quiet, so Phase 2 needs more engagement than any other phase, not less.

  • After the Big Shift: "Type SHIFT if that just landed for you."
  • After every Hell: "How many of you here have lived that one? Give me a YES." Or, "Drop a 1, 2 or 3 in the chat, which hell is yours?"
  • After every Heaven: "Type HEAVEN if you want that."
  • While they use your Event Magnet™: ask them to type what they've just written or circled.
  • The Confidence Check: "Drop a YES if you can see how this changes [their outcome]."

The rule for this phase is simple: never teach for more than a few minutes without asking the room for something.

04

Right and wrong

Heaven

✕ Not that

"You'll feel more confident and in control."

✓ Do this

A picture of an ordinary day with the problem gone, described so they can see it.

Hell

✕ Not that

"Most people struggle with getting clients."

✓ Do this

"Right now you check your phone at 7am hoping someone booked."

The Confidence Check

✕ Not that

Asking for a YES, getting silence, and ploughing on to the next slide.

✓ Do this

Slowing down, re-landing the struggle in their words, and asking again.

A quiet room at this point is telling you something. Carry on into the pitch without that YES and you're pitching to people who haven't arrived yet.

05

The steps

  1. Write your Big Shift. One sentence, said like a law.
  2. Name your three sections. Take them from the three parts of your Event Magnet™, or its three main teaching points.
  3. Write a Hell for each. A scene, in their words.
  4. Write the teaching point for each. What it is and why it matters. Stop before the how.
  5. Write a Heaven for each. A picture or an analogy.
  6. Write the "what you'll hold" line. "For the first time, you can…"
  7. Write your Confidence Check, and decide what you'll say if the room is quiet.
  8. Choose your story bit. Chapter 8 has the library.

06

Mistakes to avoid

  • Teaching the how. If someone could leave the room and run your Magic Formula™ from what you showed, you've turned a buyer into a do-it-yourselfer. The win comes from your Event Magnet™, not from a lecture.
  • Teaching more than one piece. One piece, three angles. Chapter 3 explains why.
  • Heaven as an adjective. If they can't see it, they can't want it.
  • Skipping the Confidence Check. It's the only way you'll know whether they're with you before you pitch.
  • Revealing your whole Magic Formula™ here. That belongs at the opening of Phase 3.

07

Check your understanding

  1. What is the shape of every teaching section? Hell, the teaching point, Heaven.
  2. What do you do if the Confidence Check gets silence? Slow down, re-land the struggle in their words, and ask again.
  3. What do you leave out of Phase 2? The how, and your full Magic Formula™.
