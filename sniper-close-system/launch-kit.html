Sniper Close™ Launch Kit

Everything you need to build your own Sniper Close™ presentation, in three steps: fill in the workbook, build your script, build your slides.

You'll leave with the first draft of your presentation script, written in four phases, and a slide deck built from it.

Start here

Three Steps, One at a Time

You don't need to be technical. If you can copy, paste and answer questions, you can do this.

There are three steps. Do them in order, and finish one before you open the next. Each step below tells you exactly what to click, what you'll see, and how you know you're done.

1Fill in the workbook

Your answers, in one place. No AI yet. Just you and a document.

2Build your script

An AI asks you questions, one at a time, and writes your presentation from your answers.

3Build your slides

A second AI turns your finished script into your slide deck.

What you need

  • An account with Claude (claude.ai) or ChatGPT (chatgpt.com). Either works. Claude is the easier of the two for Step 3, because it shows your slides right beside the chat.
  • A document to type into. Google Docs or Word is perfect.
  • A quiet stretch of time for each step. You don't have to do all three in one day.
If today you only do one thing

Do Step 1. The other two go quickly once your answers are written down.

Step 1

Fill In the Workbook

The workbook is every "Your action" from the training, in order, with a space for your answer. The Script Builder in Step 2 asks you for these same answers, so this is where the real work gets done.

The Sniper Close™ WorkbookCopy it, then paste it into a Google Doc or a Word document.
  1. Press Copy on the workbook above.
  2. Open a new Google Doc or Word document and paste it in (Ctrl + V on Windows, Cmd + V on a Mac).
  3. Go chapter by chapter and type your answer under each "Your answer".
  4. Use rough words. Write the way you'd say it to a friend. Don't polish anything.
  5. Don't have something yet? Type "don't have this yet" and move on. Gaps are fine.
  6. Save the document. Keep it open for Step 2.

You're done when: every chapter has an answer or a "don't have this yet" under it.

Step 2

Build Your Script

The Script Builder is a set of instructions you hand to an AI. It then interviews you and writes your presentation in four phases: WHO, WHAT, WHY and WHEN. It never makes up a story, a result or a number for you.

The Sniper Close™ Script Builder

It asks about your event, your room, your story, the one piece you teach and your offer. Then it writes one phase at a time and waits for your OK. Ten proven story bits are built in, and it picks the ones that match what your room believes.

Paste the whole prompt as it is. The length is instructions for the AI, not work for you.

  1. Open Claude or ChatGPT in a new tab and start a new chat.
  2. Come back to this page and press Copy the prompt.
  3. Click in the chat's message box, paste, and press Enter. It's long. That's normal: it's instructions for the AI, not reading for you.
  4. It asks you one question. Type your answer and press Enter. Keep your workbook open beside it and copy your answers across.
  5. Keep answering. If you don't have something, type "I don't have this yet" and it marks the gap.
  6. It shows you a short summary and asks if anything needs fixing. Fix it, or type "go".
  7. Before each phase it tells you which stories it chose, and why. Type "keep", or "swap" and say which one.
  8. It writes Phase 1 and stops. Read it. Type "keep", or tell it in plain words what to change.
  9. Do the same for Phases 2, 3 and 4.
  10. At the end it sends your script in three messages. Type "continue" after the first and the second. The third is a list called STILL TO FILL.
  11. Copy all three messages into one new document and save it.

You're done when: you have one saved document with four phases in it and a STILL TO FILL list at the bottom.

You can stop and come back

Do one phase per sitting if that suits you. Your chat is saved in your chat history, so open the same chat again and type "let's carry on".

Step 3

Build Your Slides

The Slide Builder does two things. First it makes your own set of slide moulds: thirteen slide shapes in your colours, each on a light and a dark background. Then it builds your deck from them. It doesn't write or change a word of your script. What you say sits behind each slide as your notes, and the question for the room sits at the bottom of it.

Want to see what a set of moulds looks like first? Look at the example moulds.

Claude or ChatGPT · a new chat
AI tool · prompt

The Sniper Close™ Slide Builder

It asks three quick questions, makes your moulds, then asks for your script and builds your deck.

Paste the whole prompt as it is. The length is instructions for the AI, not work for you.

  1. Start a brand-new chat. Don't use the same chat as Step 2.
  2. Press Copy the prompt here, paste it into the chat, and press Enter.
  3. Answer three quick questions, one at a time: your business name, your main colour (a plain colour name like "navy" is fine), and light or dark.
  4. It makes your moulds. In Claude they open in a panel beside the chat, with a download option on that panel. If all you see is code, type: "Give me this as a file I can download."
  5. Look through them. Type "keep", or tell it what to change ("a darker blue").
  6. Save your moulds file. It's yours to keep, and you can reuse it for every deck you make.
  7. When it asks for your script, open your saved script, select everything from PHASE 1 to the end of PHASE 4, copy it, and paste it in.
  8. It builds your deck. If it stops part-way, type "continue".
  9. Save the deck to your computer and double-click it. It opens in your web browser.
  10. Try it out. The arrow keys move between slides. N shows what to say. F goes full screen. G shows every slide at once.

You're done when: you have two saved files, your moulds and your deck, and you can click from your first slide through to your send-off.

Changing a word later

Change it in your script first. Then go back to the Slide Builder chat and say which slide to rebuild. That way your script and your slides always match.

Help

If You Get Stuck

What's happeningWhat to do
The AI stopped part-way throughType "continue".
It asked several questions at onceType "one question at a time, please".
It wrote something you never told itType "I didn't give you that. Take it out and mark it as needed."
You don't like how a part soundsTell it in plain words: "make this shorter", "say it the way I'd say it", "use my words for the pain".
It asks about your Event Magnet™ and you don't have oneSay so. It marks the place and carries on. That's the one piece the kit doesn't build, and the one to get before you go live.
You've run out of messages for nowCome back later, open the same chat, and type "let's carry on".
It all feels like a lotStop where you are. One step, or one phase, per sitting is plenty.
Remember

Your first draft doesn't have to be good. It has to exist. Read it out loud once, fix what you hear, and run it.
