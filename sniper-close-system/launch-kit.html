Sniper Close™ Launch Kit

Three things to start building your own Sniper Close™ presentation today: a workbook, a script builder and a slide builder.

You'll leave with the first draft of your presentation script, written in four phases, and a slide deck built from it.

01

How the Kit Works

Work through it in this order. Each one feeds the next.

1Fill in the workbook

One action from every chapter, plus the fill-in lines for each phase. Your answers are what the builder asks you for.

2Run the Script Builder

It asks one question at a time, then writes your presentation one phase at a time.

3Run the Slide Builder

Paste your finished script and it builds your deck, without changing a word.

One thing the kit doesn't build

Your Event Magnet™, the one-page tool your presentation is built around. The Script Builder asks what tool you have. If you don't have one yet, it marks the place and carries on, and that's the piece to get before you go live.

02

The Workbook

Every "Your action" from the training in one place, with the fill-in lines for the four phases. Do this first. It makes the builder fast.

The Sniper Close™ WorkbookOpen it in any notes app or paste it into a doc.

03

The Script Builder

This writes the first draft of your presentation: WHO, WHAT, WHY and WHEN, beat by beat. Every beat comes with what's on the slide, what you say, and what you ask the room.

Claude or ChatGPT · a new chat
AI tool · prompt

The Sniper Close™ Script Builder

It asks about your event, your room, your story, the one piece you teach and your offer, one question at a time. Then it writes one phase per turn and waits for your OK. Ten proven story bits are built in, and it picks the ones that match what your room believes. It never makes up a story, a result or a number.

Paste the whole prompt as it is. The length is instructions for the AI, not work for you.

  1. Open a new chat in Claude or ChatGPT.
  2. Paste the prompt and send it.
  3. Answer one question at a time. Rough answers are fine.
  4. Approve each phase before it writes the next.
  5. Save the finished script. It lists anything still to fill at the end.

04

The Slide Builder

This turns your finished script into one slide deck you open in your browser. Your notes sit behind each slide, and the question for the room sits at the bottom of it.

Claude or ChatGPT · a new chat
AI tool · prompt

The Sniper Close™ Slide Builder

It asks for your script, your business name, your main colour, and light or dark. Then it builds one file: your deck. It never writes or changes your copy.

Paste the whole prompt as it is. The length is instructions for the AI, not work for you.

  1. Open a new chat in Claude or ChatGPT.
  2. Paste the prompt and send it.
  3. Paste your finished script when it asks.
  4. Answer three quick questions about the look.
  5. Save the file and open it in your browser. Arrow keys move, N shows your notes, F goes full screen.
